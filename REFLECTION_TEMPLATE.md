# Problem Set 6: My reflection (Groups 5 and 6)

- **Name (Surname Firstname, for example Oppenheimer Robert):**
- **Group:** 5 or 6
- **Date:**

About half a page in all: two to four sentences under each heading is plenty. Please write in your own words, name
real runs, files and teammates' checks, and save this file as `PS6_Surname_Firstname_reflection.md` with your own
names, for example `PS6_Oppenheimer_Robert_reflection.md`. Send it in the same eLearn message as your JSON file from the self-assessment form.

## R1: Who did what

How did your team divide the work between people and agents? Name your own part.

## R2: A catch

Describe one moment when a teammate's check found something an agent, or you, had missed.

## R3: A disagreement

Where did your team, or your team and an assessor agent, disagree about the quality of a brief, and how did you
settle it?

## R4: Before a month of mornings

What would your team change before letting these agents run every morning for a month?

## R5: For the class

What will you show the class on Wednesday, and why that?
