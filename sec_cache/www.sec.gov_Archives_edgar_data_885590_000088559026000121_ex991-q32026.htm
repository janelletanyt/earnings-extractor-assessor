LAVAL, QC, October 28, 2026 – Bausch Health Companies Inc. (NYSE:BHC)(TSX:BHC) ("Bausch Health" or the "Company" or "we" or "our") today announced its third quarter 2026 financial results and other key updates from the quarter.

"Bausch Health, excluding Bausch + Lomb, delivered another solid quarter, marking fourteen consecutive quarters of year-over-year Revenue and Adjusted EBITDA growth. We also achieved an important milestone, with quarterly Adjusted EBITDA, excluding Bausch + Lomb, exceeding $1 billion for the first time. Our performance reflects disciplined execution across the portfolio, with healthy cash generation supporting more than $500 million of net debt reduction during the quarter. These results give us confidence to again raise our full year 2026 guidance. We remain focused on operational excellence, sustainable growth, and long-term value creation," said Thomas J. Appio, Chief Executive Officer, Bausch Health.

The Company also announced that its Board of Directors has authorized a $50 million share repurchase program, with the primary objective of offsetting dilution associated with the anticipated vesting of management equity awards in 2027.

Third Quarter 2026 Revenue Performance

Total consolidated reported revenues were $2.94 billion for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $2.68 billion in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of $260 million, or 10%. Excluding the impact of acquisitions of $37 million and divestitures and discontinuations of $11 million, revenue increased 8% on an organic1 basis compared with the third quarter of 2025. The net impact of foreign exchange was de minimis in the quarter.

Salix Segment
Salix segment reported revenues were $831 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $716 million for the third quarter of 2025, an increase of $115 million, or 16%. Segment revenues increased 14% on an organic1 basis compared with the third quarter of 2025. Xifaxan® was the primary contributor to growth, with 16% revenue growth in the third quarter of 2026.

International Segment
International segment reported revenues were $299 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $286 million for the third quarter of 2025, an increase of $13 million, or 5%. Excluding the impact of foreign exchange of $4 million, segment revenues grew 4% on an organic1 basis compared with the third quarter of 2025, led by Canada.

Solta Medical Segment
Solta Medical segment reported revenues were $165 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $140 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of $25 million, or 18% aided by the acquisition of our full service distributor in China. Excluding a $2 million favorable impact from foreign exchange and acquisitions of $29 million, segment revenues decreased by 4% on an organic1 basis compared with the third quarter of 2025 with the Asia Pacific region decreasing modestly.

Diversified Segment
Diversified segment reported revenues were $271 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $258 million for the third quarter of 2025, an increase of $13 million, or 5%. Segment revenues increased 5% on an organic1 basis compared with the third quarter of 2025. The increase in organic1 revenue was primarily driven by double-digit growth in Dermatology.

Bausch + Lomb Segment
Bausch + Lomb segment reported revenues were $1.38 billion for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $1.28 billion for the third quarter of 2025, an increase of $94 million, or 7%. Excluding the impact of foreign exchange of $6 million, acquisitions of $8 million and divestitures and discontinuations of $2 million, segment revenues increased 7% on an organic1 basis compared with the third quarter of 2025.

Organic Revenue (non-GAAP) and Change in Organic Revenue (non-GAAP)
Organic revenue (non-GAAP) and Change in organic revenue (non-GAAP), are defined as GAAP Revenue and change in GAAP Revenue (the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures), adjusted for changes in foreign currency exchange rates (if applicable) and excluding the impact of recent acquisitions, divestitures and discontinuations, as defined below.
Organic revenue (non-GAAP) is impacted by changes in product volumes and price. The price component is made up of two key drivers: (i) changes in product gross selling price and (ii) changes in sales deductions. The Company uses organic revenue (non-GAAP) and change in organic revenue (non-GAAP) to assess performance of its reportable segments, and the Company in total. The Company believes that providing these non-GAAP measures is useful to investors as they provide a supplemental period-to-period comparison.

The adjustments to GAAP Revenue to determine Organic Revenue (non-GAAP) and Change in Organic Revenue (non-GAAP) are as follows:
•Foreign currency exchange rates: Although changes in foreign currency exchange rates are part of our business, they are not within management’s control.
