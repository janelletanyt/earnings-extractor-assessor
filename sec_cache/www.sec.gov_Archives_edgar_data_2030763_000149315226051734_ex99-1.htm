DataMEDS AI Reports 50% Sequential Revenue Growth in Third Quarter 2026 From Second Quarter 2026, Delivers Net Profit Per Share of $2.72 and Provides Business Update

 

■Third quarter 2026 revenue of $2.7 million, up 50% from $1.8 million in the second quarter of 2026, primarily driven by growth in Corexa Pharmacy drug dispensing
■Launch of the Health Lives Here mobile app, partnered with the NFL Alumni Health marketing platform, gives Corexa Pharmacy a unique customer acquisition channel to dispense FDA-approved GLP-1 medications and the medical food Forzet™ for the dietary management of muscle loss associated with weight loss therapies
■Exclusive license to the REBOOT GLP-1 patient management platform and intellectual property creates moat to scale customer acquisition and increase long-term customer value through patented, AI-assisted patient management services
■Corexa Pharmacy launch of compounded peptides under FDA’s Enforcement Discretion Policy adds an incremental revenue opportunity
■MOU with HMO to deploy the Health Lives Here mobile app to 25,000+ members provides institutional customer to optimize PMPM model institutional customers
■Acquisition of Helomics cancer diagnostics laboratory, coupled with Humana Medicaid coverage for proprietary ChemoFx® Assay, adds incremental proprietary revenue stream, in addition to traditional lab services revenue, and opens cancer pharmaceuticals market for Corexa Pharmacy
■Company regains full compliance with Nasdaq listing standards

 

Tampa, Fla. – Monday, November 16, 2026 - DataMEDS AI, Inc. (“DataMEDS AI,” “DataMEDS” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: MEDS), a healthcare company that integrates its health information technology and physical infrastructure with its EinsteinRx™ artificial intelligence platform and PharmacyChain™ blockchain-enabled smart contracts platform, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and provided a business update.

 

“Corexa Pharmacy drove a 50% sequential increase in revenue this quarter with continued growth expected for the fourth quarter based upon the steps we have taken to add additional revenue streams of medical foods and diagnostic lab tests related to patients to whom we dispense prescriptions,” said Gerald Commissiong, Interim Co-CEO of DataMEDS. “Health Lives Here, backed by NFL Alumni Health, brings patients to Corexa Pharmacy. The REBOOT license gives us patented, AI-assisted tools to keep GLP-1 patients engaged, proprietary Tollo Health medical foods for muscle loss associated with weight loss therapies gives them unique tools to mitigate the drugs’ side effects and Helomics adds diagnostic testing revenue streams. With our first HMO relationship and compounded peptides opening new channels, and full Nasdaq compliance restored, we believe DataMEDS is well positioned for continued growth going forward.”

Business Update

 

■Health Lives Here and NFL Alumni Health. The Company launched the Health Lives Here mobile application, partnered with the NFL Alumni Health marketing platform. The app gives Corexa Pharmacy a unique customer acquisition channel to dispense FDA-approved GLP-1 medications and Forzet™, a medical food for the dietary management of muscle loss associated with weight loss therapies.
■REBOOT GLP-1 license. In October 2026, DataMEDS obtained an exclusive license to the REBOOT GLP-1 patient management platform and related intellectual property. The Company believes these patented, AI-assisted patient management services create a moat that supports scaling customer acquisition and increasing long-term customer value.
■Compounded peptides. In November 2026, Corexa Pharmacy launched compounded peptides under FDA’s Enforcement Discretion Policy published in the Federal Register on October 30, 2026, adding an incremental revenue opportunity. Compounded drugs are not FDA-approved and have not been evaluated by FDA for safety or efficacy.
■First HMO relationship. In November 2026, the Company signed a non-binding memorandum of understanding with a Florida-based HMO to deploy the Health Lives Here app to its 25,000+ members, providing a platform to optimize user engagement for an institutional customer base. Definitive agreements are expected in the fourth quarter of 2026.
■Helomics. In September 2026, DataMEDS acquired Helomics Corporation, a Pittsburgh-based CLIA-certified cancer diagnostics laboratory. Humana’s South Carolina Medicaid coverage policy for the proprietary ChemoFx® Assay, effective July 22, 2026, adds an incremental proprietary revenue stream and opens the cancer pharmaceuticals market for Corexa Pharmacy. Additionally, Helomics’ biobank of 70,000+ primary tumor biopsies creates drug discovery opportunities. The Company is adding additional testing services to Helomics’ test menu to allow it to support patient groups beyond cancer, especially GLP-1 patients.
■Nasdaq compliance. On October 19, 2026, the Company regained full compliance with Nasdaq listing standards, including the net equity requirement.

 

Profit from Operations:

 

The Company recorded a net operating profit of $10.535 million in the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared with a net operating loss of $34.318 million in the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The net profit in the third quarter of 2026 as compared with the net loss in the third quarter of 2025 loss was largely due to the extinguishment of debt and outstanding liabilities related to litigation associated with prior management and a reduction in expenses associated management compensation, as well as a reduction in general and administrative expenses. Net profit per share in the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was $2.72 on 3.961 million weighted average shares outstanding, compared to the quarter ended September 30, 2025, where the Company delivered a loss of $23.07 per share on 1.488 million weighted average shares outstanding.

About DataMEDS AI

 

DataMEDS AI, Inc. (NASDAQ: MEDS), formerly Wellgistics Health, Inc., is a healthcare company focused on the vertical integration of technology, pharmacy, pharmaceutical-adjacent and telemedicine business units through its health information technology platforms to deliver a better experience for consumers and providers. Headquartered in Tampa, Fla., DataMEDS incorporates its EinsteinRx™ artificial intelligence platform and PharmacyChain™ blockchain-enabled smart contracts platform into the Health Lives Here mobile application from Tollo Health, LLC, and its pharmacy-related Corexa Health subsidiary provides pharmacy dispensing and pharmacy-related services, including distribution of products developed by Tollo Health. DataMEDS also owns Helomics Corporation, a Pittsburgh, PA-based commercial clinical molecular diagnostics laboratory focused on the ChemoFx® Assay chemotherapy selection diagnostics test that aims to help oncologists make chemotherapy treatment decisions. Learn more at https://datamedsai.com.
