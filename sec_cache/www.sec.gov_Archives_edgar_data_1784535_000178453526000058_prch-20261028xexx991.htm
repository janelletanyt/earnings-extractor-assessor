Porch Group Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Insurance Services Revenue Growth of 39% YoY; Reciprocal Policies Written¹ Growth of 42% YoY

SEATTLE, October 28, 2026 (BUSINESS WIRE) – Porch Group, Inc. (“Porch,” “the Company,” “we,” “our,” “us”) (NASDAQ: PRCH), a new kind of homeowners insurance company, today reported third quarter results through September 30, 2026, that exceeded our expectations. As a result, the Company raised guidance for the remainder of the year.
CEO Summary
“Porch continues to execute at a high level. In Q3, we rapidly grew Reciprocal Policies Written¹ 42% year-over-year, more than doubled Adjusted EBITDA (Excluding Reciprocal)² versus the prior-year period, generated strong Adjusted EBITDA (Excluding Reciprocal)² conversion to cash for Porch shareholders, further improved our leverage ratio to the low end of our targeted range, and raised our full-year outlook again. Importantly, Insurance Services is operating as a Rule of 80 business in a soft insurance market, reinforcing our confidence that the model we have built can perform across market cycles. Our proprietary data, disciplined underwriting, expanding distribution, and largely fixed cost base are creating a powerful combination of growth and operating leverage.
“With that organic foundation firmly in place, we are excited to further accelerate our growth through the pending acquisition of Allied Trust. This is a high-quality insurance business that would add scale, products, geographic reach, an experienced team, and more than a decade of operating data. We believe Porch can make those assets even more valuable over time through our proprietary property data, distribution network, and insurance operating capabilities. Allied Trust is expected to strengthen an already compelling growth profile and position Porch for a meaningful step forward in 2027,” said Matt Ehrlichman, Chief Executive Officer, Chairman and Founder.

Third Quarter 2026 Key Financial Highlights
•Consolidated revenue was $143.9 million, up 13% YoY. Porch-Owned Segments Revenue (Excluding Reciprocal)², representing the revenue contributions across the Porch-owned businesses, was $140.8 million, up 22% YoY, led by Insurance Services revenue of $102.8 million, up 39% YoY.
•Net income attributable to Porch was $12.2 million.
•Adjusted EBITDA (Excluding Reciprocal)² of $44.5 million grew 116% YoY, largely driven by $161.0 million of Reciprocal Written Premium (“RWP”)¹ and 42% YoY growth in Reciprocal Policies Written¹.
•Consolidated gross profit was $107.9 million. Porch-Owned Segments Gross Profit (Excluding Reciprocal)², representing the gross profit contributions across the Porch-owned businesses, grew 28% YoY to $120.8 million.

Third Quarter 2026 Operational Highlights
•Top-of-funnel expansion continued, with Q3 2026 producing agency branch locations rising 114% from Q3 2025 and quote volumes rising 79% from Q3 2025.
•Conversion continued to track well above prior-year levels, supporting Q3 2026 RWP¹ from new customers growth of 178% YoY.
•Reciprocal Policies Written¹ grew 42% YoY, an acceleration in the rate of YoY growth relative to Q2 2026 (38% YoY).
•Capacity remained strong with Q3 2026 statutory surplus at the Porch Reciprocal Exchange (the “Reciprocal”) of $174.4 million, up $22 million versus Q3 2025.

On October 13, 2026, we entered into a Stock and Membership Interest Purchase Agreement pursuant to which the Reciprocal will acquire Allied Trust Insurance Company, and our wholly owned subsidiary, Homeowners of America Holding Corporation, will acquire Allied Underwriters, LLC and Lonestar Lagniappe Reinsurance Holdings, LLC, in each case subject to certain adjustments and customary closing conditions. The transaction has not closed as of the date of this release and, accordingly, no amounts related to the acquisition have been recognized in our condensed consolidated financial statements as of September 30, 2026, other than an immaterial amount of professional fees.
Under the terms of the agreements, the Reciprocal will acquire Allied Trust’s insurance carrier for an initial base price of $55 million in cash, subject to adjustments for the carrier’s statutory surplus and indebtedness at closing, plus $50 million of deferred consideration payable over the 12 months following closing, which the Reciprocal may elect to pay in cash or the largely non-admitted PRCH shares it holds. The carrier is expected to have approximately $60 million of statutory surplus at closing. After giving effect to the acquired statutory surplus, the Reciprocal’s effective net purchase consideration is expected to be approximately $45 million2, subject to closing adjustments. The transaction is expected to be approximately statutory surplus neutral for the Reciprocal. Separately, Porch Group will acquire Allied Trust’s managing general agency and captive reinsurance entity for an aggregate base purchase price of $95 million, consisting of $75 million in cash and an expected $20 million in PRCH shares. Based on Allied Trust’s expected Adjusted EBITDA (Excluding Reciprocal) contribution during the first full year following close, the transaction represents an acquisition multiple of less than four times for Porch Group. The closing will not occur earlier than January 1, 2027, and the Company currently expects the acquisition to close in the first quarter of 2027.

Adjusted EBITDA (Excluding Reciprocal)

The measure previously defined as “Adjusted EBITDA (Loss)” has been renamed to “Adjusted EBITDA (Excluding Reciprocal)” to more clearly reflect its composition. We define Adjusted EBITDA (Excluding Reciprocal) as net income (loss) adjusted for net income (loss) attributable to the Reciprocal; interest expense; income taxes; depreciation and amortization; gain or loss on extinguishment of debt; other expense; other income; impairments of intangible assets and goodwill; gain or loss on reinsurance contract; impairments of property, equipment, and software; stock-based compensation expense and employer payroll tax related to PRSU vestings; mark-to-market gains or losses recognized on changes in the value of contingent consideration arrangements, unexercised warrants, and derivatives; restructuring and other costs; acquisition and other transaction costs; and non-cash bonus expense.
Beginning in the second quarter of 2026, we revised our definition of Adjusted EBITDA (Excluding Reciprocal) to exclude employer payroll tax expense related to the vesting and settlement of our performance restricted stock units ("PRSUs"). Our PRSUs vest upon the completion of a specified service period and the achievement of distinct multi-year performance goals. The first such vesting and settlement events occurred in 2026. As a result, we incurred employer payroll tax expense related to PRSUs for the first time in 2026, and no comparable expense was present in any prior period. We have historically excluded stock-based compensation expense from Adjusted EBITDA (Excluding Reciprocal), and we believe excluding the associated employer payroll tax expense on PRSUs ongoing is consistent with that treatment. The amount of this expense is driven by the price of our common stock at the time the PRSUs vest and settle and by the episodic timing of those events upon achievement of multi-year performance goals, each of which is subject to unpredictable fluctuations outside of our control. Accordingly, we do not consider this expense to be representative of our core operating results. Because this expense first arose in 2026, no prior-period amounts were recast, and this change had no effect on any prior period presented.

Key Performance Measures and Operating Metrics
In the management of these businesses, we identify, measure and evaluate various operating metrics. The key performance measures and operating metrics used in managing the businesses are discussed below. These key performance measures and operating metrics are not prepared in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”) and may not be comparable to or calculated in the same way as other similarly titled measures and metrics used by other companies.
Insurance Services & Reciprocal Segments
Reciprocal Written Premium (“RWP”) — We define RWP as the total premium written by the Reciprocal for the face value of one year’s premium gross of cancellations, plus surplus contributions and policy fees, and before deductions for reinsurance in the period. RWP excludes the impact of cancellations and premiums ceded to reinsurers and includes surplus contributions and policy fees, and, therefore, should not be used as a substitute for revenue. We use RWP to manage the business because we believe it represents the business volume generated by associated customer acquisition activities and is reflective of the competitive market position when evaluated on a per written policy basis and is a key driver of both Porch and the Reciprocal’s growth and profit opportunities.
Reciprocal Policies Written — We define Reciprocal Policies Written as the number of new and renewal insurance policies written during the period by the Reciprocal Segment.
RWP per Policy Written — We define RWP per Policy Written as the RWP in the period, which is reflective of the total amount a policyholder is expected to pay, divided by the Reciprocal Policies Written in the period.
Software & Data
Average Number of Companies — We define Average Number of Companies as the average number of companies during the period across all of our Software & Data segment. This only includes the number of companies in our Software & Data segment.
Annualized Average Revenue per Company — We define Annualized Average Revenue per Company as the revenue generated across the Software & Data segment in the period over the Average Number of Companies in the period, which is then annualized (for example, for a given quarter, multiplied by 4).
Consumer Services
Monetized Services — We define Monetized Services as the total number of services from which we generated revenue, including, but not limited to, new and renewing warranty policies, completed moving jobs, sold security, TV/Internet or other home projects, measured over the period. This only includes services from Consumer Services segment and does not include insurance policies sold.
Average Revenue per Monetized Service — We define Average Revenue per Monetized Service as total Consumer Services segment revenue generated in the period over the number of Monetized Services.
