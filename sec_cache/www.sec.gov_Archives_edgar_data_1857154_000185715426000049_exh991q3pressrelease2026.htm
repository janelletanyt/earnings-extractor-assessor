KRISPY KREME REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS,
INCREASES REVENUE GUIDANCE AS SIGNIFICANT TURNAROUND PROGRESS CONTINUES

CHARLOTTE, NC (November 5, 2026) – Krispy Kreme, Inc. (NASDAQ: DNUT) (“Krispy Kreme”, “KKI”, or the “Company”) today reported financial results for the quarter ended September 27, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights (vs Q3 2025)
•Net revenue of $328.8 million was 12.4% lower reflecting the Company’s previously announced refranchising deals
•Systemwide sales of $490.2 million increased 1.0% in constant currency
•GAAP net loss of $13.3 million improved $6.8 million
•Adjusted EBITDA was $35.3 million, an increase of 12.7% excluding the $9.3 million cybersecurity insurance recovery recognized in the prior year period; including the recovery, Adjusted EBITDA decreased 13.1%.
•Year-to-date cash provided by operating activities of $18.7 million increased $29.8 million, and free cash flow of ($10) million improved $82 million, when compared to the first three quarters of 2025

“We made significant progress on our turnaround in the third quarter, growing systemwide sales through international franchise expansion, reducing leverage, and improving free cash flow compared to last year,” said Krispy Kreme CEO Josh Charlesworth.

“Our results demonstrate the success of the actions we are taking to drive sustainable, profitable growth and improve profitability, including a significant expansion in Adjusted EBITDA margin of 240 basis points compared to last year when excluding the cyber insurance recovery. We remain focused on successfully executing our turnaround plan and as a result are confident in our decision to increase and narrow our 2026 revenue guidance range.”

Turnaround Plan
The Company’s comprehensive turnaround plan, announced in August 2025, is designed to deleverage the balance sheet and deliver sustainable, profitable growth. The four components of the plan, along with progress on each, are as follows:
1)Refranchising: Improve financial flexibility through refranchising international markets and the joint venture in the western U.S.
a.Completed refranchising of Japan and the joint venture in the western U.S. in March 2026.
b.Entered into a development agreement to open 33 doughnut shops across the New York City metropolitan area over the next five years.
2)Improving Return on Invested Capital: Reduce capital intensity by using existing assets and focusing on franchise development.
a.Capital expenditures decreased 64.6% in the first three quarters of 2026 compared to the year-ago period.
b.Opened 98 doughnut shops around the world in the first three quarters of 2026, nearly all of which are franchised.
c.Entered into agreements for three new international franchise markets in the first three quarters of 2026, in Estonia, Mauritius, and the Netherlands.
d.Subsequent to the third quarter end, the Company entered into a development agreement with a subsidiary of Gray Mackenzie Restaurants International to enter Pakistan and develop 120 Krispy Kreme doughnut shops over the next five years. This marks the fourth international franchise development agreement in 2026.
3)Expanding Margins: Expand margins through greater operational efficiency, including outsourcing U.S. logistics.
a.Third quarter consolidated Adjusted EBITDA margin of 10.7%, was 10 basis points lower than the third quarter of 2025. Excluding the $9.3 million cybersecurity insurance recovery in the third quarter of 2025, Adjusted EBITDA margin increased from 8.3% to 10.7%, driven by a 320 basis point increase in the U.S. segment.
b.Completed outsourcing of U.S. logistics in April 2026, which has increased cost predictability and reduced complexity in our U.S. fresh delivery channel.


4)Driving Sustainable, Profitable Growth: Pursue U.S. growth based upon sustainable and profitable revenue streams. Fresh delivery is inclusive of both Company- and franchise-operated doors in the U.S.
a.Fresh delivery sales in the U.S. returned to growth, driven by increased doors.
b.Increased fresh delivery doors by 526 in the U.S. in the first three quarters of 2026, and added 715 net new doors year over year.
c.Third quarter average revenue per door per week (“APD”) was $606, a 1.5% decrease year over year.

Third Quarter 2026 Consolidated Results (vs Q3 2025)
Krispy Kreme’s results reflect continued progress in improving U.S. profitability and wider adoption of the capital-light international franchise model.

Net revenue was $328.8 million, a decline of 12.4% or $46.5 million, due primarily to refranchising the western U.S. joint venture with WKS Restaurant Group (“WKS”) and the Japan refranchising. Organic revenue decreased by 0.1%, primarily driven by a decline in the International segment, mostly offset by growth in the Market Development and U.S. segments. Global points of access increased 1,038, or 7.0%, primarily driven by the addition of 715 net doors in our U.S. fresh delivery business. Systemwide sales increased 1.0% in constant currency to 490.2 million.

GAAP net loss improved to $13.3 million, compared to the prior year third quarter net loss of $20.1 million. Diluted loss per share improved to $0.08, compared to a diluted loss per share of $0.11. Adjusted net income was $9.9 million, an improvement from Adjusted net income of $1.4 million in the prior year third quarter, and Adjusted EPS was $0.06, compared with an Adjusted EPS of $0.01.

Adjusted EBITDA decreased 13.1% to $35.3 million compared to the prior year third quarter. Adjusted EBITDA margin decreased to 10.7% from 10.8%, due primarily to lapping the $9.3 million cybersecurity insurance recovery in the prior year third quarter, mostly offset by productivity initiatives and SG&A savings. Excluding the $9.3 million cybersecurity insurance


recovery in the year ago period, Adjusted EBITDA increased $4.0 million, and Adjusted EBITDA margin improved 240 basis points, driven by a 320 basis point increase in the U.S. segment.

Diluted weighted average common shares outstanding were 176.2 million, compared to 172.6 million for the prior year third quarter.

Third Quarter 2026 Segment Results (vs Q3 2025)
U.S.: In the U.S. segment, net revenue declined by 25.6% to $160.9 million, driven by refranchising efforts associated with our turnaround plan. Organic revenue increased by 0.9% year over year, driven by growth in fresh delivery and digital.

U.S. Adjusted EBITDA decreased by 34.0% to $13.9 million and Adjusted EBITDA margin declined approximately 110 basis points to 8.6%, primarily driven by the $9.3 million cybersecurity insurance recovery in the prior year third quarter and refranchising transactions completed in the first quarter of 2026. Excluding the $9.3 million cybersecurity insurance recovery in the year ago period, Adjusted EBITDA increased $2.2 million, and Adjusted EBITDA margin improved 320 basis points, driven by increased profitability associated with our turnaround plan.

International: In the International segment, net revenue decreased by 12.0% to $123.5 million, due primarily to refranchising Japan, partially offset by foreign currency translation impacts. Organic revenue decreased by 5.4%, due primarily to declines in the U.K, partially offset by an increase in Canada.

International segment Adjusted EBITDA decreased by 13.2% to $20.1 million driven by the refranchising of Japan. Adjusted EBITDA margin decreased by 20 basis points to 16.3%.

Market Development: In the Market Development segment, net revenue increased by 135.3% to $44.4 million, driven primarily by the significant positive impact of the refranchisings in the first quarter of 2026. Organic revenue increased by 14.0%, due primarily to growth in royalty revenues in the U.S., Middle East, Japan and Brazil.

Market Development Adjusted EBITDA increased by 54.0% to $18.5 million, primarily due to the WKS Refranchising and the Japan refranchising. Adjusted EBITDA margin decreased 2,190 basis points to 41.6%, driven by changes in the regional mix of increased lower-margin U.S. franchised sales, associated with the refranchising transactions.

Balance Sheet and Capital Expenditures
During the first three quarters of 2026, the Company spent $28.6 million, or 2.8% of net revenue, on capital expenditures, as it continues to primarily invest in repairs and maintenance of existing infrastructure, while leveraging excess capacity for growth where available. Year to date, the Company’s capital expenditures declined 64.6% versus $80.8 million in the first three quarters of 2025.

As of the end of the third quarter of 2026, the Company’s net leverage ratio was 5.5x, reflecting a 1.2x reduction compared to end of the fourth quarter of 2025. The Company had total available liquidity of $250.5 million as of September 27, 2026, which includes $30.3 million of cash and cash equivalents as well as undrawn capacity of $220.2 million under its credit facilities. The Company remains in compliance with all financial covenants as of September 27, 2026.

Refranchising
Krispy Kreme set a goal of completing two to three international refranchising deals in 2026. The Company completed the refranchising of Japan and is pursuing at least one more international refranchising deal this year. The Company completed the refranchising of the western U.S. joint venture with WKS. As Krispy Kreme evaluates additional refranchising opportunities, it remains focused on identifying the right partners both in international markets and the U.S. to maximize value and position the Company for long-term growth. Additionally, Krispy Kreme entered into a development agreement with an existing franchisee to open 33 doughnut shops across the New York City metropolitan area over the next five years.

For fiscal 2025, approximately 25% of the Company’s systemwide sales came from franchise-operated locations. During the third quarter, approximately 42% of systemwide sales are generated through franchised locations. Through additional refranchising efforts, the Company’s goal remains to reach approximately 50% of systemwide sales generated by franchisees beginning fiscal 2027.

Definitions
The following definitions apply to terms used throughout this press release:
•Systemwide Sales: Reflects global sales in U.S. dollars on a nominal basis of all Krispy Kreme products, whether operated by the Company or franchisees, excluding mix, equipment, and royalty revenue. Sales from franchisees are reported to the Company by such franchisees and are not included in Company revenues. Growth in systemwide sales represents the change in one period from the same period in the prior year on a constant currency basis. The Company believes systemwide sales information is important because it is indicative of the health of the Company’s brand and aids in understanding the Company’s financial performance.
•Global Points of Access: Reflects all locations at which fresh doughnuts can be purchased. The Company defines global points of access to include all Hot Light Theater Shops, Fresh Shops, Carts and Food Trucks, and fresh delivery doors (which includes Krispy Kreme branded cabinets and merchandising units within high traffic grocery and convenience stores, quick service or fast casual restaurants, club memberships, and drug stores), and other points at which fresh doughnuts can be purchased at both Company-owned and franchise locations as of the end of the applicable reporting period. The Company monitors global points of access as a metric that informs the growth of our omni-channel presence over time and believe this metric is useful to investors to understand our footprint in each of our segments and by asset type.
•Hubs: Reflects locations where fresh doughnuts are produced and processed for sale at any global point of access. The Company defines hubs to include self-sustaining Hot Light Theater Shops and Doughnut Factories, at both Company-owned and franchise locations as of the end of the applicable reporting period.
•Hubs with Spokes: Reflects hubs currently producing fresh doughnuts for other Fresh Shops, Carts and Food Trucks, or fresh delivery doors, and excludes hubs not currently producing fresh doughnuts for other shops, Carts and Food Trucks, or fresh delivery doors.
•Sales Per Hub: Sales per hub equals fresh revenues from hubs with spokes, divided by the average number of hubs with spokes at the end of each of the five most recent quarters.
•Fresh Revenues from Hubs with Spokes: Fresh revenues is a measure focused on the Krispy Kreme doughnut business and includes product sales generated from our Hot Light Theater Shops, Fresh Shops, Carts and Food Trucks, fresh delivery doors, and digital channels and excludes sales from Cookie Bakeries and Branded Sweet Treats (through the date of the Insomnia Cookies Holdings, LLC (“Insomnia Cookies”) deconsolidation and Branded Sweet Treats exit, respectively). Fresh revenues from hubs with spokes equals the fresh revenues derived from hubs with spokes.
•Free Cash Flow: Defined as cash provided by operating activities less purchases of property and equipment.

About Krispy Kreme
Headquartered in Charlotte, N.C., Krispy Kreme is one of the most beloved and well-known sweet treat brands in the world. Our iconic Original Glazed® doughnut is universally recognized for its hot-off-the-line, melt-in-your-mouth experience. Krispy Kreme operates in more than 40 countries through its unique network of fresh doughnut shops, partnerships with leading retailers, and a growing digital business. We define “Adjusted EBITDA” as earnings before interest expense, net, income tax expense, and depreciation and amortization, with further adjustments for share-based compensation, certain strategic initiatives, acquisition and integration expenses, and certain other non-recurring, infrequent, or non-core income and expense items. Adjusted EBITDA, both on a consolidated and at the segment level, is a principal metric that management uses to monitor and evaluate operating performance and provides a consistent benchmark for comparison across reporting periods. “Adjusted EBITDA margin” reflects Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of net revenues.
We define “Adjusted net income/(Loss), diluted” as net loss attributable to common shareholders, Adjusted for share-based compensation, certain strategic initiatives, acquisition and integration expenses, amortization of acquisition-related intangibles, the tax impact of adjustments, and certain other non-recurring, infrequent, or non-core income and expense items. “Adjusted EPS” is Adjusted net income/(Loss), diluted converted to a per share amount.
Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA margin, Adjusted net income/(Loss), diluted, and Adjusted EPS have certain limitations, including adjustments for income and expense items that are required by GAAP. In evaluating these non-GAAP measures, you should be aware that in the future we will incur expenses that are the same as or similar to some of the adjustments in this presentation, such as share-based compensation. Our presentation of these non-GAAP measures should not be construed to imply that our future results will be unaffected by any such adjustments. Management compensates for these limitations by relying on our GAAP results in addition to using these non-GAAP measures supplementally.
