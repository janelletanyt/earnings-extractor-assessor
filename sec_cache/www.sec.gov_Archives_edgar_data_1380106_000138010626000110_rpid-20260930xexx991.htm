Rapid Micro Biosystems Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
•Reports third quarter 2026 total revenue of $9.5 million, representing 22% growth compared to the third quarter of 2025
◦Placed 7 Growth Direct systems and completed 12 validations in the third quarter of 2026
•Reports record third quarter 2026 total gross margin of 25%, an increase of 16 percentage points compared to the third quarter of 2025
◦Product gross margin increased 19 percentage points to 12%, and Service gross margin increased 14 percentage points to 54%, compared to the third quarter of 2025
•Today announced that the Company has signed a definitive agreement to be acquired by Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany
Lexington, MA, November 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Rapid Micro Biosystems, Inc. (Nasdaq: RPID) (the “Company”), an innovative life sciences technology company providing mission critical automation solutions to facilitate the efficient manufacturing and fast, safe release of healthcare products, today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
"We are pleased to report 22% third quarter total revenue growth driven by both our Product and Service businesses," said Rob Spignesi, President and CEO of Rapid Micro Biosystems. "I am also particularly pleased with the continued progress in our gross margin expansion initiatives, which resulted in a third quarter total gross margin of 25%, including record Product and Service gross margins."
"I'm incredibly proud of what the Rapid Micro team has accomplished and the impact the Growth Direct platform is having across pharmaceutical manufacturing globally," continued Spignesi. "Over the past 18 months, we have built a strong partnership with Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany centered on a shared commitment to innovation and helping customers modernize quality control workflows. We are confident that joining the Life Science business of Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany will significantly expand the Growth Direct's reach, accelerate adoption of the Growth Direct platform and more fully serve our combined global customer base."
Third Quarter Financial Results
Total revenue for the third quarter of 2026 increased 21.6% to $9.5 million, compared to $7.8 million in the third quarter of 2025. The Company placed seven new Growth Direct systems and completed the validation of 12 customer systems, compared to five placements and four validations in the third quarter of 2025. Product revenue increased 24.9% to $6.5 million, compared to $5.2 million in the third quarter of 2025. Service revenue increased 14.9% to $3.0 million, compared to $2.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. Recurring revenue increased 6.9% to $5.2 million, compared to $4.8 million in the third quarter of 2025.
Total cost of revenue was $7.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $7.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. Third quarter gross margin of 25.2%, or $2.4 million, was a quarterly record, compared to 8.9%, or $0.7 million in the third quarter of 2025.
Total operating expenses increased 8.1% to $13.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $12.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. Third quarter 2026 operating expenses included a non-recurring restructuring charge of $0.7 million associated with the focused efficiency program announced by the Company in August 2026. General and administrative expenses increased by 12.2%, research and development expenses decreased by 15.8%, and sales and marketing expenses increased by 4.3%, in each case compared to the third quarter of 2025.
Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $11.3 million and net loss per share was $0.23, compared to a net loss of $11.5 million and net loss per share of $0.26 in the third quarter of 2025.
Non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $7.9 million, compared to a loss of $9.4 million in the third quarter of 2025.
Cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments were approximately $7 million as of September 30, 2026.


Definitive Agreement to be Acquired by Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany
In a separate press release, the Company announced that it has signed a definitive agreement pursuant to which Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany, has agreed to acquire, subject to the terms and conditions set forth in the agreement, all outstanding shares of Rapid Micro Biosystems. For additional details regarding this announcement, please refer to the related press release at https://investors.rapidmicrobio.com/news-and-events/news-releases.
In light of the acquisition announcement, the Company will not host a conference call to discuss third quarter 2026 financial results and is withdrawing its full year 2026 guidance.
As a result of the additional time required to complete certain financial reporting and disclosure matters in connection with the acquisition announcement, the Company will delay the filing of its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The Company intends to file its Form 10-Q within the extension period permitted under Rule 12b-25 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934.
About Rapid Micro Biosystems
Rapid Micro Biosystems is an innovative life sciences technology company providing mission critical automation solutions to facilitate the efficient manufacturing and fast, safe release of healthcare products such as biologics, vaccines, cell and gene therapies, and sterile injectables. The Company’s flagship Growth Direct system automates and modernizes the antiquated, manual microbial quality control (“MQC”) testing workflows used in the largest and most complex pharmaceutical manufacturing operations across the globe. The Growth Direct system brings the quality control lab to the manufacturing floor, unlocking the power of MQC automation to deliver the faster results, greater accuracy, increased operational efficiency, better compliance with data integrity regulations, and quicker decision making that customers rely on to ensure safe and consistent supply of important healthcare products. The Company is headquartered in Lexington, Massachusetts and has U.S. manufacturing in Lowell, Massachusetts, with global locations in Switzerland, Germany, and the Netherlands. For more information, please visit www.rapidmicrobio.com or follow the Company on X (formerly known as Twitter) at @rapidmicrobio or on LinkedIn.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
In addition to reporting financial measures in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (“GAAP”), the Company is including in this press release "non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA," which is a non-GAAP financial measure. The Company defines non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA as net loss excluding interest income, interest expense, other expense, net, income tax expense, depreciation and amortization, stock-based compensation, and restructuring.
The Company includes this non-GAAP financial measure because it believes it allows investors to understand and evaluate the Company’s core operating performance and trends. In particular, the exclusion of certain items in calculating non-GAAP adjusted EBITDA can provide useful measures for period-to-period comparisons of the Company’s core business. This non-GAAP financial measure has limitations as an analytical tool, including the fact that such non-GAAP financial measure may not be comparable to a similarly titled measure presented by other companies because other companies may calculate non-GAAP EBITDA differently than the Company does. For more information regarding this non-GAAP financial measure, see the table included at the end of this press release.
