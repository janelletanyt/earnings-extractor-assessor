Workhorse Group Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

DETROIT, November 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Workhorse Group Inc. (NASDAQ: WKHS) ("Workhorse" or the "Company"), an American Industrial Technology company, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 and provided a business update.

“The third quarter not only marked continued execution on our core 2026 integration and synergy creation goals, but it saw the continued impact of that execution on driving growth,” said Scott Griffith, CEO of Workhorse. “We enter the fourth quarter full of momentum in both our Commercial Vehicles and Modular AI Infrastructure lines of business.”

The Company has spent the first three quarters of 2026 continuing progress on its key priorities for the year: completing the integration, expanding the product portfolio and building the sales backlog. The majority of key integration goals (systems, personnel, facilities) are complete, and based on third quarter results, the Company has realized approximately $13.8 million of synergies in the first nine months of 2026, and expects to exit 2026 at or above its $20 million target.

Engineering, design and development are underway on the Company’s next generation modular chassis, which the Company expects will be key to not only reducing bill of material (BOM) costs, but also increasing the range of vehicles that can be built from it. In addition, heading into Q4 2026 and Q1 2027, the Company expects to deliver a Company-record number of electric step vans while continuing to grow its sales pipeline. In addition, Workhorse recently announced a strategic partnership with RHAELM to jointly commercialize TerraPod™, Workhorse’s new modular data center platform.

“Our evolution into an American Industrial Technology company becomes more clear every day” Griffith continued. The team has done an amazing job of executing on the logistical and technical challenges of integration, while increasing production of vehicles and taking steps to launch an entirely new product line – all in the space of less than a year. You can always count on a Workhorse”

Third Quarter and Recent Strategic Highlights

•Merger Integration on Track: Integration is nearly complete across all priorities, including enterprise systems, office locations, teams and management. The company is preparing a new integrated office in the Bay Area to house engineering, sales and service staff, and to serve as a capabilities demonstration center and as a hub for customer engagement. Based on third quarter results, the Company is operating at an annualized cost synergy run rate of approximately $21 million, has realized approximately $13.8 million of synergies in the first nine months of 2026, and expects to exit 2026 at or above its $20 million target.

•Modular AI Data Center Progress: Modular AI Data Center Progress: In October, Workhorse announced a strategic partnership with RHAELM to jointly commercialize TerraPod™, the Company’s new modular data center platform. Under a Manufacturing Spec Unit Purchase Agreement, RHAELM will pay Workhorse $10 million to build a full-scale TerraPod manufacturing spec unit at Workhorse's Union City, Indiana facility. The companies also signed a non-binding Letter of Intent that sets a path to sign a definitive Supply Agreement, which is framed to include a first order provision for 150 TerraPod systems. The companies are targeting execution of the Supply Agreement by December 31, 2026.



•Bill of Materials Cost Reduction Advancing: Engineering and design work on the Company's modular chassis and next generation powertrain program is progressing. The modular chassis is expected to not only help reduce Bill of Material costs, but also to enable a greater number of truck types to be built atop it, which the Company believes will open up a wider swath of the medium-duty segment.

•Continuing the Production Ramp: Workhorse increased production in the third quarter and is in the midst of one of the largest production runs in its history, with contracted orders supporting higher production volumes into early 2027.

•Continued W56 Demand and Dealer Network Expansion: Customer demand for the W56 continued to build. In September, Gateway Fleets, a California-based provider of turnkey electric vehicle and charging solutions for commercial delivery operators, placed a follow-on purchase order for 200 W56 electric step vans, building on its 2026 order of 100 W56s. Deployments of the new order are expected to begin in the second half of 2027 and continue through 2028. In October, Workhorse expanded its dealer network by signing its first Canadian dealer agreement, with Tristan Fleet Management, to sell and service the W56 line in Canada.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

•Revenue: Sales, net of returns and allowances, for the third quarter of 2026 were $6.1 million, an increase of 72% from $3.6 million in the second quarter of 2026. For the first nine months of 2026, revenue was $14.0 million, up 22% from $11.5 million in the first nine months of 2025 on a GAAP basis. Compared to the third quarter of 2025, revenue was $6.1 million versus $9.6 million on a GAAP basis and $12.0 million on a pro forma combined basis. On a pro forma combined basis, revenue for the first nine months of 2025 was $20.2 million.

•Vehicles Delivered: The Company delivered 39 vehicles during the third quarter of 2026, compared to 26 vehicles in the second quarter of 2026 and 38 vehicles in the third quarter of 2025 on a GAAP basis.

•Operating Expenses: Total operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $11.1 million, a 7% decrease from $11.9 million in the second quarter of 2026. Selling, general and administrative expenses were $7.4 million, compared to $7.8 million in the second quarter. Research and development expenses were $3.7 million, compared to $4.1 million in the second quarter. Compared to total operating expenses of $7.9 million in the third quarter of 2025, the 2026 results reflect the full combined company cost base.

•Interest Expense: Net interest expense was $1.4 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $0.8 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $5.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. The sequential increase reflects higher borrowings under the Company’s credit agreements to fund working capital and operations.

•Cost of Sales: Cost of sales for the third quarter of 2026 was $15.7 million, resulting in a gross loss of $9.6 million, compared to a gross loss of $7.5 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $1.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. The Company continues to expect gross margin to improve as production volumes at Union City scale and the cost benefits of the combined platform are realized.

•Operating Loss: Operating loss was $20.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $19.4 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $9.3 million in the third quarter of 2025.


•Net Loss: Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $21.8 million, or $2.00 per basic and diluted share, compared to a net loss of $20.2 million, or $1.86 per share, in the second quarter of 2026 and $14.9 million, or $1.55 per share, in the third quarter of 2025.

•Capital Position: As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $4.9 million in cash and cash equivalents, plus $0.5 million of restricted cash. During the first nine months of 2026, the Company drew $30.0 million under its Cash Flow Credit Agreement, including $10.0 million during the third quarter, and drew $20.0 million under its Customer Order Credit Agreement and repaid $1.6 million. Subsequent to quarter end, in October 2026, the Company amended the Credit Agreements to, among other things as detailed in our most recent public disclosures, increase the borrowing capacity of the Customer Order Credit Agreement from $20.0 million to $30.0 million, and immediately borrowed the additional $10.0 million available to enable us to continue manufacturing vehicles to meet customer demand.

About Workhorse Group Inc.

Headquartered in the Detroit area, Workhorse Group Inc. (Nasdaq: WKHS) is an American industrial technology company that designs, engineers, and produces the intelligent industrial machines that are the lifeblood of modern economies. From its manufacturing facility in Union City, Indiana, Workhorse applies its electrification, high-voltage systems integration, ruggedized platform manufacturing and distributed energy capabilities to its electric commercial vehicles and modular data center products. * Note on Financial Statement Presentation

On December 15, 2025, we completed our merger with Motiv. While the legal acquirer in the merger was Workhorse, for financial accounting and reporting purposes under U.S. GAAP, Motiv was the accounting acquirer, and the Merger was accounted for as a reverse acquisition. Accordingly, the consolidated assets, liabilities and results of operations of Motiv became the historical consolidated financial statements of the consolidated company, and Workhorse's assets, liabilities and results of operations were consolidated with those of Motiv beginning on December 15, 2025. As a result, comparative third quarter and nine-month 2025 financial information reflects only Motiv and is not directly comparable to the combined company results for the third quarter and nine months of 2026.
