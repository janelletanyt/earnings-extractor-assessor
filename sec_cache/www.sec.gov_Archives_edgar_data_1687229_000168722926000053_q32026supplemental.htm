Earnings Press Release
Invitation Homes Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Dallas, TX, October 28, 2026 — Invitation Homes Inc. (NYSE: INVH) (“Invitation Homes,” “we,” “our,” and “us”), the nation’s premier single-family home leasing and management company, today announced our Third Quarter (“Q3”) 2026 financial and operating results.

Q3 2026 Highlights
•Year over year, total revenues increased 6.3% to $731 million, property operating and maintenance costs increased 2.0% to $264 million, and net income available to common stockholders increased 15.6% to $158 million, or $0.27 per diluted common share.
•Year over year, Core FFO per share increased 2.4% to $0.48, while AFFO per share increased 0.1% to $0.38.
•Same Store NOI increased 1.1% year over year on 1.6% Same Store Core Revenues growth and 2.5% Same Store Core Operating Expenses growth.
•Same Store Average Occupancy was 96.4%, a reduction of 10 basis points year over year.
•Same Store renewal rent growth of 4.3% and Same Store new lease rent growth of (0.2)% resulted in Same Store blended rent growth of 3.0%.
•We sold 658 wholly owned homes, most to families purchasing for their own use, and acquired 216 wholly owned homes, for net dispositions of 442 homes and net proceeds of approximately $245 million that we used to fund share repurchases.
•As previously announced, on July 8, 2026, we closed a public offering of $500 million aggregate principal amount of 4.950% senior notes (the “Notes”). The Notes were priced at 99.291% of the principal amount and mature on February 1, 2032. Net proceeds were used to prepay a portion of our secured debt obligation maturing in June 2027.

Capital Allocation Update
From December 1, 2025 through October 27, 2026, we bought back nearly $1 billion of our shares. This includes an aggregate of 36,923,753 shares repurchased to date at an average stock price of $26.78, and represents an implied value per home of approximately $280,000 against a gross average sales price of approximately $460,000 for homes sold during that same period.

On October 26, 2026, our board of directors authorized an additional $500 million share repurchase program.

Comments from Chief Executive Officer Dallas Tanner
“Our third quarter reflected steady execution across the platform. Same Store NOI grew 1.1% year over year, blended rent growth was 3.0%, and with the benefit of our share repurchases, Core FFO per share grew 2.4%. Since December 2025, we’ve repurchased nearly $1 billion of our stock at a substantial valuation discount. We believe that is more than any other residential REIT. With the new $500 million share repurchase program our board just authorized, we intend to keep funding buybacks with home sale proceeds whenever our stock trades at a meaningful discount to the value of our homes, guided by market conditions and a disciplined approach to the balance sheet.”

“Following a solid third quarter, we are raising our full year guidance midpoints for Core FFO per share by $0.01 to $1.96, Same Store NOI growth guidance to 1.2%, and wholly owned home sales to $950 million, which supports our continued focus on closing the gap between public and private market values.”

Same Store NOI
For the Same Store Portfolio of 76,687 homes, Same Store NOI for Q3 2026 increased 1.1% year over year on Same Store Core Revenues growth of 1.6% and Same Store Core Operating Expenses growth of 2.5%.

YTD 2026 Same Store NOI increased 0.9% year over year on Same Store Core Revenues growth of 1.7% and Same Store Core Operating Expenses growth of 3.3%.

Same Store Core Revenues
Q3 2026 year over year Same Store Core Revenues growth of 1.6% was primarily driven by a 1.8% increase in Average Monthly Rent and a 2.6% increase in other property income, net, partially offset by a 10 basis point year over year decrease in Average Occupancy and a 10 basis point year over year increase in bad debt.

YTD 2026 year over year Same Store Core Revenues growth of 1.7% was primarily driven by a 2.0% increase in Average Monthly Rent and a 4.1% increase in other property income, net, partially offset by a 40 basis point year over year decrease in Average Occupancy.

Same Store Core Operating Expenses
Q3 2026 year over year Same Store Core Operating Expenses increased 2.5%, primarily attributable to a 4.9% increase in controllable expenses and a 1.1% increase in fixed expenses.

YTD 2026 year over year Same Store Core Operating Expenses increased 3.3%, primarily driven by a 4.7% increase in controllable expenses and a 2.5% increase in fixed expenses.

Investment, Property Management, and Homebuilding Activity
During Q3 2026, we sold 658 wholly owned homes, most to families purchasing for their own use, for gross proceeds of approximately $318 million, and we sold 18 homes for gross proceeds of approximately $7 million in our joint ventures. Acquisitions for Q3 2026 included 216 wholly owned homes for approximately $74 million and 288 homes for approximately $92 million in our joint ventures.

YTD 2026, we sold 1,798 wholly owned homes for gross proceeds of approximately $833 million and 42 homes for gross proceeds of approximately $18 million in our joint ventures. We also acquired 673 wholly owned homes for approximately $239 million and 375 homes for approximately $122 million in our joint ventures.

On September 15, 2026, we executed a series of agreements to effectuate the dissolution of the Pathway joint ventures, Pathway Property Company (“Pathway Homes”) and Pathway Operating Company. We were the sole limited partner and provided all of the equity capital to Pathway Homes from its inception. In connection with the transaction, we acquired the general partner and all other interests in Pathway Homes, resulting in our ownership of a controlling financial interest in the entity and the consolidation of the 894 homes, along with other assets and liabilities, previously owned by Pathway Homes. Those homes are now reported as wholly owned homes. In conjunction with the transaction, the operating agreements governing the Pathway joint ventures were terminated, resulting in the dissolution of Pathway Operating Company, the technology and asset management platform that served the homes. During Q3 2026, we recognized a non-cash net loss of $30.4 million related to the dissolution of the joint ventures and the resulting consolidation of the assets and liabilities at fair value, which is excluded from Core FFO, AFFO, and Adjusted EBITDAre.
