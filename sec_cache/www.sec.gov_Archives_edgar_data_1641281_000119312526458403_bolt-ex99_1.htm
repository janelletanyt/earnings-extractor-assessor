Bolt Biotherapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update

 

•
Completed acquisition of Prana Therapies and $120 million concurrent private placement financing, establishing PRA-216 and the INSPIRE-built pipeline as Bolt’s primary development focus
•
Initiated Phase 2 studies with PRA-216, a half-life-extended IL-4Rα/TSLP bispecific, in asthma, atopic dermatitis and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease with initial data expected in 2Q 2027
•
Recently disclosed PRA-216 Phase 1 data provided first clinical validation of PRA-216 and the proprietary INSPIRE multispecific antibody platform with the potential for at-home maintenance dosing every 8 to 12 weeks; a second wholly owned INSPIRE program is expected to enter the clinic and generate initial clinical data in 2H 2027
•
Cash runway expected to fund operations into 2028 through multiple clinical and preclinical milestones

 

REDWOOD CITY, CA, November 12, 2026 – Bolt Biotherapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: BOLT), a clinical-stage biotechnology company advancing next-generation, half-life-extended multispecific antibodies, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.

 

In October, Bolt completed its acquisition of Prana Therapies in a sign-and-close, stock-for-stock reverse merger and closed a concurrent $120 million private placement financing with leading healthcare institutional investors. Following the transaction, Prana’s pipeline became the primary development focus of the company, led by flagship program PRA-216, an IL-4Rα/TSLP bispecific antibody in Phase 2 development across multiple high-value inflammatory indications. In connection with the transaction, Sheila Gujrathi, M.D., Co-Founder and former Chief Executive Officer of Prana, was appointed Chief Strategy Officer of Bolt and joined the company’s Board of Directors.

 

“The transaction and financing provide us with the capital and public-company foundation to advance PRA-216 and the broader INSPIRE pipeline through what we believe will be an exciting and highly data-rich period ahead,” said Sheila Gujrathi, M.D., Chief Strategy Officer of Bolt Biotherapeutics. “PRA-216 is our flagship program and the first clinical validation of the INSPIRE platform. It is now in Phase 2 development in asthma, COPD and atopic dermatitis, with an additional study planned in eosinophilic esophagitis, and we expect patient proof-of-concept data across all four studies beginning in the second quarter of 2027. These studies will evaluate the potential of PRA-216 to move beyond the efficacy ceiling of single-mechanism therapies by combining complementary IL-4Rα and TSLP inhibition, while offering the potential convenience of infrequent, at-home dosing. Beyond PRA-216, we look forward to advancing additional wholly owned programs from INSPIRE as we work to build a new generation of differentiated multispecific therapies.”

 

“Completing the Prana acquisition marks an important transformation for Bolt,” said Willie Quinn, President and Chief Executive Officer of Bolt Biotherapeutics. “With a strong balance sheet, an advanced clinical program and a differentiated platform, we believe we are well positioned to execute against the important milestones ahead.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

•
Cash and Financing– In October 2026, subsequent to the end of the third quarter, Bolt completed a private placement financing resulting in approximately $120 million in gross proceeds. The financing is expected to fund the company’s operations into 2028 and through multiple anticipated clinical and preclinical milestones.
•
Collaboration Revenue – Total collaboration revenue was zero for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $2.2 million for the same quarter in 2025. Revenue in the comparative periods was generated from services performed under the R&D collaborations as we fulfill our performance obligations.
•
Research and Development (R&D) Expenses – R&D expenses were $2.2 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $6.5 million for the same quarter in 2025. The decrease between the comparable periods was mainly due to a R&D tax incentive, a decrease in salary and related expenses primarily as a result of our restructuring, and overall lower research and development activities.
•
General and Administrative (G&A) Expenses – G&A expenses were $2.5 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $3.3 million for the same quarter in 2025. The decrease between the comparable periods was mainly due to a decrease in salary and related expenses primarily as a result of our restructuring as well as lower personnel expenses.
•
Loss from Operations – Loss from operations was $4.7 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $7.7 million for the same quarter in 2025.

Subsequent Events

•
Merger with Prana Therapies: Bolt acquired Prana on October 23, 2026 through the issuance of 430,746 shares of common stock and 16,360.929 shares of Series D non-voting preferred stock, each share of which is convertible into 1,000 shares of Common Stock. Bolt will call and hold stockholders’ meetings as soon as practicable to approve the conversion of the Series D Preferred Stock into Common Stock, and other matters as specified in the Merger Agreement. Bolt will provide the disclosures required by ASC 805, Business Combinations, once they are ready and no later than January 8, 2027.
•
Private Placement: Concurrent with the acquisition of Prana, Bolt sold and issued 13,995.311 shares of Series D Preferred Stock and accompanying warrants for aggregate gross proceeds of $120.0 million (not including any cash proceeds that may be received upon the exercise of such warrants), before deducting placement agent fees and other offering expenses.
•
Shares Outstanding: After the acquisition of Prana and the Private Placement, subject to stockholder approval of the conversion of the Series D non-voting convertible preferred stock, Bolt is expected to have approximately 34.2 million shares of common stock outstanding on an as-converted basis, before giving effect to the exercise of the PIPE warrants
•
Lease Termination: Following the October 2026 acquisition of Prana, the Chesapeake Master Lease has been terminated with an effective lease expiration date of January 1, 2027.

About Bolt Biotherapeutics, Inc.

Bolt Biotherapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company pioneering a new generation of half-life-extended multispecific antibodies designed to deliver best-in-disease therapies. Through the proprietary INSPIRE platform it acquired from Prana Therapies, the company combines complementary therapeutic mechanisms in fully IgG-based molecules designed for differentiated efficacy, extended exposure, and convenient dosing.

 

 

The company’s lead program, PRA-216, is a novel IL-4Rα/TSLP bispecific antibody currently in Phase 2 development in asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, and atopic dermatitis, with a fourth Phase 2 study planned in eosinophilic esophagitis. In addition to PRA-216, Bolt Biotherapeutics is advancing a wholly owned pipeline of additional INSPIRE-based multispecific programs across diseases with significant unmet need.

 

Bolt’s pipeline also includes BDC-4182, a claudin 18.2-targeting Boltbody™ ISAC.
