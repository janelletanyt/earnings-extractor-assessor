AsiaFIN Holdings Corp. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results, Reporting Strong Nine-Month Financial Performance with Improved Margins

 

KUALA LUMPUR, MY / ACCESS Newswire / November 16, 2026 / AsiaFIN Holdings Corp. (OTCQB:ASFH), a global financial ecosystem enabler, today announced its financial results for the third quarter of 2026 ended September 30, 2026, reporting strong year-to-date revenue growth with an improved gross margin of 35.5%.

 

Financial Results for the Quarter and the Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026:

 

●Cash and cash equivalents (including fixed deposits) were approximately $1.47 million as of September 30, 2026 as compared to approximately $1.75 million as of December 31, 2025.

 

●Revenue for the quarter was approximately $1.17 million, a decrease of 25.6% compared to approximately $1.58 million in the third quarter of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, revenue increased by approximately $759,000, or 23.7%, to $3.96 million, compared with $3.20 million in the corresponding period of 2025.

 

●Gross profit for the quarter was approximately $305,000, or 26.0% gross margin, compared to approximately $737,000, or 46.8% gross margin, in the third quarter of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, gross profit increased by 34.9% to $1.41 million, compared with $1.04 million in the corresponding period of 2025. Gross profit margin also improved to 35.5%, from 32.5% in 2025, as revenue increased at a higher rate than cost of revenue.

 

●Selling, general and administrative expenses were approximately $371,000, an increase of 0.6% compared to approximately $368,000 in the third quarter of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, selling, general and administrative expenses decreased by 11.4% to $1.22 million from $1.38 million. The decrease was primarily attributable to lower credit loss allowance expenses, advertising expenses, consultancy fees and travelling expenses.

 

●Net loss was approximately $58,000 for the quarter, compared with net income of approximately $371,000 in the corresponding quarter of 2025. The quarterly performance reflects the timing of project milestone achievements and the associated revenue recognition. While the third quarter was softer than the previous year, the Company maintained positive operating income and net income results for the nine-month period. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company recorded net income of approximately $198,000, compared to a net loss of approximately $327,000 in the corresponding period of 2025.

 

●Net loss attributable to common shareholders was approximately $47,000 for the third quarter, compared to net income attributable to common shareholders of approximately $380,000 in the corresponding quarter of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, net income attributable to common shareholders was approximately $231,000, compared to a net loss attributable to common shareholders of approximately $300,000 in the same period last year.

 

●Total comprehensive loss for the third quarter was approximately $53,000, compared to total comprehensive income of approximately $390,000 in the third quarter of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company recorded total comprehensive income of approximately $180,000, compared to a total comprehensive loss of approximately $206,000 in the corresponding period of 2025. Net loss per share, basic and diluted, was $0.0006 for the third quarter of 2026, compared to net income per share of $0.0046 in the third quarter of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, net income per share was $0.0028, compared to a net loss per share of $0.0037 in the corresponding period of 2025.

 

KC Wong, AsiaFIN’s CEO, said, “AsiaFIN’s business is project-driven and seasonal, with revenue recognition typically concentrated in the fourth quarter as major project milestones are completed and signed off. However, the momentum for our recurring SaaS business continues to contribute to our business.

 

“While third-quarter revenue was lower year-over-year due to fewer project milestones achieved during the quarter, our nine-month performance remained encouraging. Revenue grew 23.7%, gross margin improved to 35.5%, and net income attributable to common shareholders reached approximately $231,000, compared with a net loss of approximately $300,000 in the same period last year.

 

“As we enter our traditionally stronger fourth quarter, we now shift our focus to the U.S. to tap this large market, starting with the states of New York and New Jersey. We are exploring synergies with local companies in this region as well as other states when opportunities arise. However, we remain attuned to the South Asian and Southeast Asian markets,” Mr. Wong concluded.

About AsiaFIN Holdings Corp.

 

AsiaFIN Holdings Corp. (OTCQB:ASFH), a Nevada corporation, operates through its wholly owned subsidiaries in Malaysia, Hong Kong, and the British Virgin Islands. AsiaFIN’s mission is to become the “global financial ecosystem enabler” through its solutions in Fintech; Regulatory Technology (RegTech); ESG Consultancy & Reporting; and Agentic and Robotic Process Automation (APA/RPA) services. AsiaFIN provides services to more than 100 financial institutions and over 100 corporate clients in the Asia and Middle East region, including Malaysia, Myanmar, the Philippines, Indonesia, Bangladesh, Pakistan, Thailand, Singapore and Saudi Arabia. AsiaFIN’s clients are central banks, financial institutions and large corporations. For further information regarding the company, please visit https://asiafingroup.com.
