StablecoinX Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results: Revenue Rises Nearly Eightfold Sequentially as DVN Surpasses $6 Billion in Cumulative Verified Volume

 

ENA treasury of approximately 3.0 billion tokens, about 20% of total supply, now unlocked; Ethena governance approves programmatic ENA buyback framework

 

NEW YORK, November 16, 2026 -- StablecoinX Inc. (Nasdaq: USDE), the first public stablecoin infrastructure company focusing on the Ethena digital dollar ecosystem, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, its first full quarter as a public company.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

 

●Revenue of $477,993 for the third quarter, a 666% increase from $62,372 in the second quarter of 2026, driven almost entirely by the Company’s Decentralized Verifier Node (DVN) infrastructure services business, reflecting the first full quarter of DVN revenue-generating activity.
   
●Net loss of $(100.2) million, or $(4.17) per share; Adjusted net loss (non-GAAP) of $(1.9) million, or $(0.08) per share.1 Net loss was driven primarily by a $91.0 million non-cash loss associated with a change in the fair value of warrant liabilities (both public and private warrants), resulting from an increase in the Company’s share price during the quarter, and a $7.3 million non-cash loss on the extinguishment of former SPAC sponsors’ notes and their conversion to private warrants.
   
●ENA treasury of approximately 3.0 billion tokens, or roughly 20% of ENA’s total supply, carried at $212.8 million on StablecoinX’s balance sheet, with a market value of approximately $802 million, or $33.37 per Class A share, based on ENA’s September 30, 2026 closing price of $0.2648. ENA’s price has declined since quarter end; see “ENA Treasury” below.
   
●Cash of $10.4 million and total assets of $226.2 million (consisting mostly of the Company’s ENA treasury carried at $212.8 million) as of September 30, 2026.
   
●DVN surpassed $6 billion in cumulative verified cross-chain volume in September, up from $3.0 billion as of August 14, 2026, the date of StablecoinX’s second quarter earnings release. The Company’s DVN went live on TRON on October 9, 2026 and is currently deployed on 34 chains and active on 22 chains.
   
●ENA lock-ups permanently waived under previously announced Waiver Letter with the Ethena Foundation and Ethena OpCo (the “Ethena parties”). The waiver was effective as of October 5, 2026 and gives StablecoinX greater balance-sheet flexibility and opens new avenues to use the Company’s ENA treasury for value-accretive activities.
   
●Leadership transition completed: appointed Christopher Jensen, a founding member of Franklin Templeton’s digital asset group, as Chief Executive Officer and a member of the Board of Directors on September 8, 2026. Edward Chen, who led StablecoinX through its public listing as Chief Executive Officer, continues as Chairman of the Board, focused on long-term strategy, capital markets positioning and the Company’s partnership with Ethena.
   
●Simplified capital structure: restructured the former SPAC sponsors’ convertible demand notes in August 2026 into private placement warrants and a cash payment approximating $343 thousand, which strengthened the Company’s liquidity position.

“Our third quarter performance provided an initial indication of the value StablecoinX was built to deliver,” said Christopher Jensen, Chief Executive Officer. “Our verifier infrastructure doubled its cumulative verified volume to more than $6 billion in less than two months, which increased transaction revenue, and our ENA treasury is now fully unlocked. Ethena’s recently approved fee switch, a programmatic ENA buyback mechanism, marks an important development for Ethena ecosystem participants. With approximately 20% of ENA’s total supply, StablecoinX gives public-market investors exposure to that ecosystem. Our priority is scaling our operating businesses to generate recurring revenue and keeping our shareholders and the investment community informed of our progress.”

 

“Taking StablecoinX public was the first step. Continuing to build the operating company behind the ENA treasury is the next,” said Edward Chen, Chairman of the Board of StablecoinX. “Since joining the Company as CEO in early September, Christopher has brought institutional rigor to how we run the business and the entire team is focused on delivering consistent growth. The senior management team and the Board are aligned on our long-term growth strategies, our capital markets positioning, and the partnership with Ethena to fundamentally grow the ecosystem that underpins everything we do.”

 

ENA Treasury

 

As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s ENA treasury totaled approximately 3.0 billion tokens. Under U.S. GAAP, these tokens are recorded as digital intangible assets at cost less impairment, with a carrying value of $212.8 million. Based on ENA’s September 30, 2026 closing price of $0.2648, the treasury had a market value of approximately $802 million, or approximately $33.37 per share based on 24,029,576 shares of Class A common stock outstanding. As of October 30, 2026, based on an ENA closing price of $0.1901, the treasury was valued at approximately $576 million, or $23.96 per share based on 24,029,576 shares of Class A common stock outstanding.

 

As previously disclosed, on September 14, 2026, StablecoinX entered into a Waiver Letter with the Ethena parties that, effective as of October 5, 2026, permanently waived all lock-up, vesting and unlocking restrictions on the Company’s ENA tokens, including the 48-month contractual lock-up on tokens acquired in the PIPE. The effective date aligns with Ethena’s acceleration of all remaining investor unlocks for other ENA holders. The Waiver Letter also establishes a framework for the Company to sell ENA to fund working capital and strategic initiatives that are value-accretive to the Ethena ecosystem, subject to advance notice and a right of first refusal for Ethena. The ENA tokens held by the Company remain subject to other contractual selling restrictions under its Collaboration Agreement with Ethena.

 

“Our ENA treasury and our operating businesses are designed to complement one another,” said Young Cho, Chief Financial Officer of StablecoinX. “This quarter we simplified our capital structure by restructuring the former sponsors’ notes, added operating revenue, and gained the flexibility of a fully unlocked treasury with a disciplined, ecosystem-aligned framework to allow for strategic and value-accretive activities. We remain focused on scaling our three business lines and narrowing the gap between the Company’s market capitalization and the value of our digital asset holdings.”

Ethena Ecosystem

 

Ethena’s USDe is one of the largest digital dollar products by market capitalization. The third quarter brought several important developments across the Ethena ecosystem:

 

●Fee switch approved. Ethena governance approved the ENA fee switch during the quarter, a programmatic ENA buyback mechanism that would initiate buybacks once the 14-day average USDe circulating supply reaches 7.5 billion. Under the approved framework, the share of protocol revenue directed to ENA buybacks increases as USDe circulating supply reaches defined milestones. StablecoinX abstained in the governance vote on the ENA fee switch.
   
●Supply recovery. USDe supply recovered from a quarterly low of $3.9 billion at the end of July 2026 to $4.9 billion at September 30, 2026, a 9.5% net increase from July 1, with supply growing $760 million in September.
   
●Protocol revenue momentum. The Ethena protocol generated $57 million in gross protocol revenue in the third quarter, with monthly revenue rising from $17.6 million in July to $21.3 million in September, and cumulative protocol revenue surpassing $850 million.
   
●Fully backed. Total backing was $4.95 billion against USDe supply of $4.94 billion at September 30, 2026, and the sUSDe 7-day average APY rose from 3.8% to 5.0% over the quarter. As previously announced by Ethena, the USDe basis trade was expanded to tokenized equities in September.
   
●Tokenomics overhang removed. The Ethena Foundation bought out locked tokens from certain seed investors and, effective October 5, 2026, accelerated all remaining original investor unlocks, leaving no investor tokens subject to lock-up. Ethena Labs and the Ethena Foundation also reached an agreement in principle on a Master Framework Agreement intended to direct the economic upside of the protocol to the Foundation and its ecosystem.
   
●Distribution expansion. USDe expanded across Robinhood Chain, Coinbase, Binance, Kraken, Morpho, Aave, Monad and TRON; Ethena Pay, a self-custodial money app, entered beta on September 1 with a staged rollout across 48 countries; FalconX joined as an institutional lending partner; and Standard Chartered initiated research coverage of Ethena.

 

Business Update and Outlook

 

●DVN. The StablecoinX DVN verifies cross-chain messages for Ethena assets. Infrastructure services generated revenue of $477,993 in the third quarter of 2026, compared with $62,372 in the second quarter of 2026. Cumulative verified volume passed $6 billion in September, and following USDe’s launch on TRON on October 9, 2026, the DVN began verifying transfers to and from the TRON network. Effective October 31, 2026, the Company exited its Ethereum validator services to focus on its core Ethena-focused infrastructure. The Company’s DVN is currently deployed on 34 chains and active on 22 chains.
   
●Infrastructure Software. StablecoinX Harness, a middleware platform that consolidates stablecoin acceptance, conversion to sUSDe, and cross-chain delivery into a single API, launched its initial phase on July 2, 2026 and signed its first client on July 10, 2026.
   
●Distribution Services. Subject to market and regulatory conditions, StablecoinX continues to plan the launch of its Distribution Services business to broaden investor access to USDe. The Company currently expects to launch these services in 2027.

 

“Looking ahead, we are excited about momentum across the Ethena ecosystem, from Ethena Pay’s rollout to Ethena’s expansion of the basis trade into tokenized equities,” said Christopher Jensen, Chief Executive Officer. “At StablecoinX, our goal is to support the growth of USDe adoption through our operating businesses and to build long-term value for our shareholders. Ethena is building momentum on its side, and we intend to build on ours, extending USDe’s reach in ways that complement the protocol and extend the ecosystem.”

About StablecoinX

 

StablecoinX Inc. (Nasdaq: USDE) is a publicly traded company offering investors exposure to the stablecoin economy through its strategic focus on the Ethena ecosystem, one of the world’s largest issuers of digital dollars. The Company operates across infrastructure services, infrastructure software, and distribution services, each purpose-built to advance and scale the Ethena ecosystem. As stablecoins increasingly serve as foundational infrastructure for global payments, decentralized finance, and digital capital markets, StablecoinX combines the accessibility of a public listing with direct participation in one of the fastest-growing segments of global finance.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

This press release presents Adjusted net loss and Adjusted net loss per share, each of which is an important financial measure for the Company but is not a financial measure defined by GAAP.

 

Adjusted net loss is calculated by taking net loss and removing the impact of the change in fair value recognized for restricted digital assets, liabilities denominated in digital assets, related party demand notes, and warrant liabilities.

 

Adjusted net loss per share is calculated by taking Adjusted net loss and dividing it by the weighted average shares used in computing net loss per share attributable to common stockholders, basic and diluted.

 

The Company believes these non-GAAP measures of financial results provide useful supplemental information to management and investors regarding certain financial and business trends related to our financial condition and results of operations, and as a supplemental tool for investors to use in evaluating our ongoing operating results and trends and in comparing our financial measures with competitors who also present similar non-GAAP financial measures. Adjusted net loss and Adjusted net loss per share have limitations and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for performance measures calculated under GAAP.

 

The financial statement tables that accompany this press release include a reconciliation of Adjusted net loss to the most comparable U.S. GAAP financial measure.

 

Current and prospective investors should review the Company’s audited annual and unaudited interim financial statements, which are filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, and not rely on any single financial measure to evaluate the Company’s business. Other companies may calculate Adjusted net loss and Adjusted net loss per share differently or may not calculate them at all, and therefore these measures may not be directly comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies.
