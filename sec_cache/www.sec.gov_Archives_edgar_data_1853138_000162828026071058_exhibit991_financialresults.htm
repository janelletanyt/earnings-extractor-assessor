Kodiak AI Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results, Secures Landmark Unsupervised Autonomous Trucking Agreement as Company Nears Long-Haul Launch

•Secured a binding definitive agreement with DTL Transport for 75 customer-owned and operated unsupervised autonomous trucks, marking a major commercial milestone for Kodiak's Driver-as-a-Service model.
•Advanced our Autonomy Readiness Measure to 99%, nearing our targeted year-end unsupervised autonomous long-haul launch.
•Delivered the first Kodiak-powered Western Star 49X trucks to Atlas Energy Solutions, bringing Atlas's fleet of customer-owned autonomous trucks to 37.
•Selected International LT as Kodiak's third OEM platform, with unsupervised autonomous deployments targeted for the second half of 2027.
•Named IKEA Supply as our first long-haul launch partner for unsupervised autonomous operations, advancing plans for regular commercial freight service.

MOUNTAIN VIEW, Calif. (November 5, 2026) - Kodiak AI, Inc. (Nasdaq: KDK), a leading provider of Physical AI-powered autonomous vehicle technology, today announced results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

During the third quarter, Kodiak made significant strides toward its targeted year-end unsupervised autonomous long-haul launch, while laying the groundwork for the next phase of commercial growth. With growing customer commitments and an expanding unsupervised autonomous fleet, the Company is building a scalable, Driver-as-a-Service business with opportunities across Long-haul trucking, Industrial and Defense. Kodiak's ability to deploy its Physical AI-powered technology across multiple truck platforms, combined with its commercial operating experience, supports its strategy to grow deployments and recurring revenue through a capital-efficient model.

“We are approaching a defining moment for Kodiak as we advance toward our targeted year-end unsupervised autonomous long-haul launch, with our Autonomy Readiness Measure now at 99%,” said Don Burnette, Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Kodiak. “Our landmark binding agreement with DTL Transport to deploy the Kodiak


Driver in 75 customer-owned and operated unsupervised autonomous trucks represents a significant commercial commitment to our Driver-as-a-Service model and demonstrates growing customer confidence in Kodiak's technology and commercial capabilities. With years of real-world commercial operating experience behind us, we're building toward the next phase of growth: more trucks, more miles, and more customer contracts.”
Third Quarter Results and Business Highlights:
•Secured a binding definitive agreement with DTL Transport to deploy the Kodiak Driver in 75 customer-owned and operated trucks, marking a landmark commercial commitment under Kodiak's Driver-as-a-Service model and building on its commercial freight operations with DTL.
•Advanced the Autonomy Readiness Measure to 99%, bringing Kodiak closer to its targeted year-end unsupervised autonomous long-haul launch and the start of regular unsupervised autonomous freight service on U.S. highways.
•Delivered the first two Kodiak-powered Western Star trucks to Atlas Energy Solutions, bringing Atlas's fleet of customer-owned unsupervised autonomous trucks to 37 by quarter-end.
•International LT selected as Kodiak's third OEM platform, with engineering integration underway and unsupervised autonomous deployments targeted for the second half of 2027.
•Named IKEA Supply as Kodiak's first unsupervised autonomous long-haul launch partner, with plans to begin regular unsupervised autonomous freight service along the Dallas-to-Houston corridor.
•Brought simulation capabilities fully in-house, including Kodiak's AI-powered BreakPoint technology, to accelerate development, testing and validation of the Kodiak Driver while reducing costs.
•Surpassed 58,000 cumulative paid driverless hours and nearly 30,000 cumulative loads delivered, while transporting more than 396,000 tons of freight during Q3, more than double the tons transported in the prior quarter.
•Launched commercial freight operations with Charger Logistics, along the Dallas-to-Laredo corridor, thereby adding another customer and route to Kodiak's long-haul network.
•Completed the West Fraser trial in Alberta, Canada, demonstrating the adaptability of Kodiak's autonomous technology to rugged forestry operations and supporting potential expansion into additional Industrial applications.
•Successfully completed the U.S. Marine Corps ROGUE-Fires demonstration at Fort Hunter Liggett, showcasing new autonomous convoy capabilities using


technology derived from Kodiak's commercial platform and highlighting its potential for broader Defense applications.
•Received a permit to test autonomous trucks in California and made the first ever autonomous delivery in the state, establishing a pathway toward future unsupervised autonomous commercial deployment in California.
•Q3 Net Cash used in operating activities of $35.2 million.
•Q3 Free Cash Flow (Non-GAAP) of negative $38.0 million.
•Ended Q3 with $113.6 million in Cash and Cash Equivalents and Marketable Securities.
“Our third quarter results reflect continued commercial progress as we build a more predictable, recurring revenue business,” said Surajit Datta, Chief Financial Officer of Kodiak. “Revenue grew 286% year-over-year, driven primarily by expanding Driver-as-a-Service operations and a one-time Defense contribution, while free cash flow performance exceeded our guidance expectations. As we prepare for the next phase of commercial growth, our priorities remain clear: grow recurring revenue, reduce the cost of the Kodiak Driver, and improve operating leverage as deployments scale.”

About Kodiak AI, Inc.
Kodiak AI, Inc. (Nasdaq: KDK) is a leader in Physical AI, developing unsupervised autonomous technology designed to power any machine that moves.
The core of the company’s solution is the Kodiak Driver, a vehicle-agnostic autonomous driving system that combines advanced AI-powered software with modular hardware. Together, they perceive, think and act in the real world. Kodiak’s technology is currently deployed by customers in active, real-world operations with no one in the cab.
The Kodiak Driver is designed to operate across long-haul trucking, industrial trucking, and defense. Because the same AI system is deployed across multiple applications, experience compounds: progress in one improves Kodiak’s Physical AI solution across all of them.
Kodiak AI commercializes its technology through Driver-as-a-Service offerings, customer-owned unsupervised autonomous deployments, and strategic partnerships. In 2024, Kodiak achieved a historic milestone, becoming the first company to deploy driverless technology in customer-owned driverless trucks. Today, its commercial partners and customers include Atlas Energy Solutions, Werner Enterprises, IKEA, Bridgestone, Roehl Transport, General Dynamics Land Systems, and C.R. England.
For more information about Kodiak, please visit https://kodiak.ai/investors.

Non-GAAP Operating Expenses and Non-GAAP Loss from Operations
We define these non-GAAP financial measures as their respective GAAP measures, each excluding stock-based compensation expense. We use these non-GAAP financial measures as part of our overall assessment of our performance, including the preparation of our annual operating budget and quarterly forecasts, to evaluate the effectiveness of our business strategies, and to communicate with our board of directors concerning our financial performance. Stock-based compensation is a non-cash expense that varies in amount from period to period and is dependent on market forces that are often beyond our control. As a result, management excludes this item from internal operating forecasts and models. Management believes that non-GAAP measures adjusted for stock-based compensation provide investors with a basis to measure our performance against the performance of other companies without the variability created by stock-based compensation as a result of the variety of equity awards used by other companies and the varying methodologies and assumptions used.
Free Cash Flow
We define free cash flow as net cash used in operating activities, which is the most directly comparable measure calculated in accordance with GAAP, less purchases of property and equipment. We believe free cash flow is a useful indicator of liquidity that provides our management, board of directors, and investors with information about our future ability to generate or use cash to enhance the strength of our balance sheet and further invest in our business and pursue potential strategic initiatives.
