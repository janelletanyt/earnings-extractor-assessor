EXHIBIT 99.1
Freenome Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

– Third-quarter results reflect revenue of $183.5 million driven by the FDA approval of SimpleScreen™ CRC –

– Entered the public market and raised gross proceeds of $310 million –


BRISBANE, Calif. (Nov. 10, 2026) — Freenome, Inc. (NASDAQ: FRNM), a personalized cancer detection company using an AI-driven multiomics platform to optimize single- and multi-cancer blood tests, today reported its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided guidance for 2026.


Third Quarter Financial Highlights

•Total revenue was $183.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, driven primarily by $180.0 million recognized under our collaboration and license agreement following FDA approval of SimpleScreen CRC, compared to $1.0 million for the third quarter of 2025.
•License and collaboration revenue was $182.5 million, and service and other revenue was $0.9 million for the third quarter of 2026.
•GAAP net income was $60.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to a GAAP net loss of $64.5 million for the third quarter of 2025.
•Went public on the Nasdaq Capital Market on July 21, 2026, under the ticker symbol FRNM, having raised approximately $310 million in gross proceeds from top healthcare investors.
•Cash, cash equivalents and marketable securities totaled $430.0 million as of September 30, 2026.
•Net cash provided by operating activities was $38.4 million for the third quarter of 2026.
•Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP financial measure, was $117.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to negative $56.3 million for the corresponding period in 2025. See “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below.
•Completed the conversion of approximately $50.5 million of principal and accrued interest under the Abbott convertible note into approximately 3.4 million shares at $15.00 per share. Upon the closing of the business combination in July 2026, the Roche convertible note was converted into approximately 6.4 million shares at $12.00 per share.

Recent Operating Highlights

•Received FDA approval on July 24, 2026, for SimpleScreen™ CRC, a blood-based screening test for adults 45 and older at average risk for colorectal cancer, triggering a $100 million milestone payment under Freenome’s collaboration and exclusive U.S. commercial license agreement with Exact Sciences (assumed by Abbott after acquisition).
•Reported topline results from an updated version of SimpleScreen™ CRC. The study met all primary and secondary endpoints and showed improved detection of advanced precancerous lesions, including lesions with high-grade dysplasia.
•Under the commercial license agreement, the Freenome-developed SimpleScreen™ CRC test was launched by Abbott on September 30, 2026 in the United States.
•Subsequent to quarter-end in October, expanded the Personalized Cancer Detection (PCD) portfolio with the launch of SimpleScreen™ Lung and the option to add SimpleScreen CRC in a single blood draw, for individuals eligible for both screenings. SimpleScreen Lung is a protein-based laboratory-developed screening test for adults ages 50 to 80 with a significant smoking history who are not currently participating in guideline-recommended screening.
•Received FDA Breakthrough Device Designation for the multiomics version of the SimpleScreen Lung test that is currently being developed.
•Advanced development of the AI-powered multiomics discovery platform for the PCD pipeline, with the next two single-cancer readouts anticipated in December and our first asymptomatic multi-cancer detection readout in mid 2027.


“Freenome made meaningful progress in its first quarter as a public company, executing on our strategic partnerships and strengthening our operating foundation,” said Aaron Elliott, Ph.D., CEO of Freenome. “With SimpleScreen CRC approved by the FDA and our lung program advancing, we remain focused on expanding access to blood-based early cancer detection. Looking ahead, we believe the opportunity is substantial as our multiomics AI platform positions us to build a portfolio of single-cancer and multi-cancer tests that has the potential to truly transform how cancer is detected.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

For the three months ended September 30, 2026, total revenue was $183.5 million, compared to $1.0 million for the same period in 2025, an increase of $182.5 million. License and Collaboration revenue was $182.5 million, compared to none in 2025. Service and Other revenue was $0.9 million, compared to $1.0 million in 2025.

Total operating costs and expenses were $113.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $66.3 million for the third quarter of 2025, an increase of $47.2 million that was primarily driven by transaction and stock-based compensation costs related to the closing of the business combination. Research and development expense was $68.4 million and general, and administrative expense was $44.6 million for the third quarter of 2026.

GAAP net income was $60.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to a GAAP net loss of $64.5 million for the corresponding period in 2025. Non-GAAP net income was $114.0 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to a non-GAAP net loss of $61.4 million for the corresponding period in 2025.

Adjusted EBITDA was $117.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to negative $56.3 million for the corresponding period in 2025.

Net cash provided by operating activities was $38.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to net cash used in operating activities of $42.0 million for the same period in 2025. Free cash flow was $35.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to negative $44.8 million for the same period in 2025. Non-GAAP net income (loss), Adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow are non-GAAP financial measures.

See “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below for definitions of these measures and the tables following the condensed consolidated financial statements for reconciliations to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures.

Freenome expects full year 2026 total revenue of $192 million to $194 million, reflecting contributions from license and collaboration agreements, research and development services, and early commercial activities.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

Freenome prepares its financial statements in accordance with U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP). To supplement its GAAP financial results, this press release also contains certain measures, on a non-GAAP basis. The non-GAAP measures presented in this release are non-GAAP net income (loss), Adjusted EBITDA and free cash flow. These non-GAAP financial measures are not prepared in accordance with, and are not alternative to, GAAP, and should be read only in conjunction with Freenome’s financial statements prepared in accordance with GAAP.

Non-GAAP net income (loss) is adjusted as shown in the reconciliation below. The adjustments include stock-based compensation and amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets. The reconciliation separately identifies the loss on remeasurement of the convertible note. Ordinary depreciation is not excluded from non-GAAP net income (loss).



Adjusted EBITDA is defined as net income (loss) adjusted for interest and investment income, interest expense, other income (expense), net, depreciation and amortization, stock-based compensation, and the convertible-note remeasurement loss, which is presented as a separate adjustment in the reconciliation. We believe Adjusted EBITDA provides supplemental information to help investors evaluate financial performance across periods. The reconciliation of GAAP net income (loss) to Adjusted EBITDA is provided below.

Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by (used in) operating activities less purchases of property and equipment in the period. Freenome believes free cash flow is a useful supplemental liquidity measure for investors because it reflects the cash generated from (used in) its operations after investment in property and equipment. Free cash flow does not represent the residual cash flow available for discretionary expenditures because it does not deduct all mandatory payments or other contractual obligations, including any required debt principal repayments. Free cash flow should not be considered in isolation from, or as a substitute for, net cash provided by (used in) operating activities, investing activities or financing activities.

Freenome believes non-GAAP net income (loss), when considered with GAAP net income (loss), is useful to investors because it provides a supplemental way to assess period-to-period changes in reported net income by showing the effects of stock-based compensation, acquisition-related intangible amortization and convertible-note remeasurement. Stock-based compensation is recurring and expected to remain a significant cost of our business.

Non-GAAP financial measures are not uniformly defined, and Freenome’s non-GAAP financial measures may be calculated differently from, and therefore may not be comparable to, similarly titled measures reported by other companies.

These non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for, or as superior to, the corresponding financial measures prepared in accordance with GAAP. Among other limitations, these financial measures exclude certain expenses, and gains and losses that are required to be recorded by GAAP and therefore do not reflect all costs of Freenome’s business.

About Freenome

Freenome is an early cancer detection company developing blood-based screening tests to identify cancer in its earliest, most treatable stages. The company’s proprietary AI-driven multiomics discovery platform analyzes circulating cell-free DNA methylation patterns at single-base resolution alongside additional biomarkers to detect multiple cancers. Freenome’s development of FDA-approved SimpleScreen CRC for colorectal cancer screening and a pipeline of tests for lung cancer and additional single- and multi-cancer indications is designed to increase cancer screening participation among millions of at-risk individuals.
