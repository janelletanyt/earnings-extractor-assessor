Stoneridge Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

Continued Sales Momentum and Cost Discipline Highlight 3Q Progress

NOVI, Mich. – November 4, 2026– Stoneridge, Inc. (NYSE: SRI) today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
2026 Third Quarter Highlights:

Continued sales growth and commercial momentum:
•Reported Q3 sales growth of 12.4% YoY to $155.8 million, which reflects a $24.2 million reduction to net sales for the PM sensor arbitration award described below. On an adjusted basis, revenue improved 29.9% to $180.0 million;
•Record quarterly revenue for MirrorEye (>$37 million) and Stoneridge Brazil ($21.5 million);
•Stoneridge Electronics secures significant additional orders for existing vision systems in the OEM market which are expected to generate approximately $180 million in cumulative incremental sales through 2028.

Disciplined execution yields improved adjusted financial results:
•SG&A as a percentage of adjusted revenue improved 317 basis points YoY to 13.1%; on track to deliver over $5 million in cost savings in 2026;
•Net loss from continuing operations of $22.9 million, or $0.81 per diluted share, versus a net loss of $8.9 million, or $0.32 per diluted share, in the prior year. Adjusted net income from continuing operations was $1.6 million, or $0.06 per diluted share, versus an adjusted net loss of $7.6 million, or $0.27 per diluted share in the third quarter of 2025;
•Adjusted EBITDA of $7.3 million, representing 41.7% growth versus the third quarter of 2025

Revising full-year guidance:
•Adjusted sales in the range of $690 million to $700 million (versus the prior range of $645 million to $670 million) on improved market demand, continued product momentum and year-to-date outperformance at Stoneridge Brazil
•Adjusted EBITDA now anticipated at the bottom end of the prior $20 million to $25 million range driven by higher transitory costs, inflationary pressures and unfavorable currency impacts
“We delivered another quarter of strong execution, with adjusted sales growth of nearly 30% and adjusted EBITDA growth of more than 40% year over year, despite increasing inflationary pressures and supply chain disruption,” said Natalia Noblet, president and chief executive officer. “During the third quarter, we also resolved several legacy customer warranty matters and completed a review of obsolete inventory across our Electronics Division. Finally, the approximate $180 million of additional vision systems business further validates our technology and strengthens our position in the OEM market. We are encouraged by our progress, and our strengthened leadership team remains intensely focused on execution, mitigating inflationary headwinds and delivering results as global vehicle production volumes recover from the cyclical trough.”

Results for the third quarter of 2026 were adversely affected by a pre-tax charge of $24.2 million, recorded as a reduction to net sales, related to an adverse ruling received on November 2, 2026 in the previously disclosed arbitration proceeding brought by a former customer of our Control Devices business regarding legacy PM sensor product warranty claims. The Company exited the PM sensor product line in 2019 and divested its Control Devices business in the first quarter of 2026. The arbitration panel awarded the former customer €22.5 million (approximately $25.5 million), including interest and costs. Because the ruling provided additional evidence about conditions that existed at September 30, 2026, the charge was recognized in third quarter results. The Company disagrees with the ruling and continues to believe the claims lack merit, and it is evaluating its options with respect to the award. However, there can be no assurance that the Company will be successful in appealing the decision and may ultimately be obligated to satisfy the award without modification. Provided that an appeal is not successful, the Company expects to pay the award from cash on hand, including cash held outside the United States, and intends to hold discussions with the former customer regarding the timing of payments.

Due to the material impact this charge had on reported third quarter results, the Company is also furnishing non-GAAP consolidated and segment-level financial measures, which it believes are useful in assessing the Company’s core operating performance. The exhibits attached hereto provide reconciliation details on normalizing adjustments of non-GAAP financial measures used in this press release.

Consolidated net sales from continuing operations was $155.8 million, an increase of 9.5% YoY, including a $24.2 million reduction to net sales for the PM sensor arbitration award. Adjusted consolidated net sales from continuing operations totaled $180.0 million, an increase of 29.9%. The North American and European commercial vehicle markets and Stoneridge Brazil were the primary contributors to growth in the third quarter.

Cash and Debt Balances
As of September 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents totaled $63.4 million with total debt of $143.7 million, resulting in net debt of $80.2 million. The $47.6 million decrease in net debt compared to December 31, 2025 reflects the deployment of proceeds from the sale of the Control Devices business in January and tighter control of working capital during the year. The Company’s outstanding borrowings under its Credit Facility are classified as a current liability and the Credit Facility matures on July 1, 2027. The company expects to refinance the Credit Facility, and is currently engaged in a global refinancing process.

2026 Outlook & Management Commentary
Based on year-to-date results and current expectations for the fourth quarter, the Company is revising its 2026 guidance ranges. “During the third quarter we made tangible progress realigning our cost structure, which should provide a durable foundation for profitable growth over the long term,” said Noblet. “Given strengthening demand across the European and North American commercial vehicle markets, which is anticipated to carry into 2027, year-to-date outperformance in our Brazilian business, and the demonstrated success of our innovative safety and efficiency enhancing technologies, we now expect 2026 adjusted revenue to exceed our prior expectations. That said, given the proactive measures we have undertaken to resolve several legacy warranty and inventory-related matters that were not envisioned in our original guidance and uncertainty created by ongoing inflationary cost pressures, in particular for semiconductors, and unfavorable currency movements, we now anticipate that full year adjusted EBITDA will come in at the bottom end of our prior expectations.”
