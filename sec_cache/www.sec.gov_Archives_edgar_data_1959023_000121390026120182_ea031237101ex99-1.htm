RenX Enterprises Reports Record Third Quarter Revenue of $4.48 Million, Up 27.5% Year Over Year

 

Third consecutive quarter of sequential revenue growth, with gross margin expanding to 34.3%; Microtec mill construction complete, with first substrate production targeted for January 2027 supporting a targeted path to Compost Sales segment breakeven in the first half of 2027

 

MIAMI, FL, November 16, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — RenX Enterprises Corp. (NASDAQ: RENX) (“RenX” or the “Company”) today reported financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026. Revenue grew sequentially for the third consecutive quarter to $4.48 million, gross margin reached its highest level of the year, and the Logistics segment posted its third consecutive quarter of operating profitability, as the Company completed construction of the Microtec mill, which is expected to begin producing engineered growing media in January 2027.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

 

●Record consolidated revenue of $4.48 million, up 5.3% sequentially and 27.5% over the third quarter of 2025, the third consecutive quarter of sequential revenue growth in 2026 ($3.96 million in the first quarter; $4.26 million in the second); revenue for the first nine months of 2026 was $12.70 million.

 

●Gross profit of $1.54 million, up 13.2% sequentially, at a 34.3% gross margin, the Company’s highest of 2026 and its third consecutive quarter above 30%.

 

●Net loss of $8.7 million, compared with $8.0 million in the second quarter of 2026. The quarter included $5.7 million of interest expense, of which approximately $4.1 million was non-cash amortization of debt discount and issuance costs. Net loss per share was $(3.27).

 

●EBITDA improved for the second consecutive quarter, to $(2.3) million from $(4.4) million in the second quarter and $(7.2) million in the first quarter of 2026, a 68% improvement from the first quarter to the third; Adjusted EBITDA was $(2.2) million in the third quarter, compared with $(1.9) million in the second quarter and $(1.6) million in the first quarter (see “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below).

 

●Logistics segment: record revenue of $3.39 million, gross margin of 25.3% (up from 21.7% in the second quarter), and operating income of $0.42 million, up 61% sequentially and the segment’s third consecutive quarter of operating profitability.

 

●Compost Sales segment: revenue of $1.09 million, up 3.9% sequentially, at a 62.1% gross margin; operating loss narrowed 16% to $(0.77) million.

 

●Cash of $0.2 million at September 30, 2026, compared with $54 thousand at December 31, 2025.

 

●Stockholders’ equity of $4.0 million at September 30, 2026.

 

●Microtec mill program: construction and installation complete at Myakka City, with approximately $1.8 million of the approximately $2.0 million program invested through September 30, 2026; energization expected before year-end 2026 and first substrate production targeted for January 2027.

 

“RenX grew revenue every quarter of 2026, expanded gross margin to its best level of the year, and delivered a third straight profitable quarter in Logistics. That is fundamental growth, quarter after quarter, generated by operations,” said David Villarreal, Chief Executive Officer of RenX Enterprises Corp. “And the platform that produced it is about to get a new engine. Construction of the Microtec mill is complete, first production is targeted for January, and in 2027 the feedstock we already receive at Myakka City begins leaving the facility as engineered growing media: locally produced, at the source of the feedstock, while freight costs keep raising the delivered price of substrate shipped into Florida from out of state.”

 

 

 

Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

Consolidated revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $4.48 million, up 5.3% from $4.26 million in the second quarter of 2026 and 27.5% from $[3.52] million in the third quarter of 2025. Revenue growth in the third quarter was driven by higher hauling volumes in the Logistics segment and continued demand for compost, soils and mulch products in the Compost Sales segment.

 

Gross profit was $1.54 million, up 13.2% sequentially, at a gross margin of 34.3%. Gross margin has held above 30% in every quarter of 2026 and expanded in the third quarter even as volumes grew. For the first nine months of 2026, gross profit was $4.23 million at a 33.3% gross margin.

 

Operating loss was $3.0 million, essentially flat with the second quarter on 5.3% higher revenue. Net loss was $8.7 million, or $(3.27) per share, compared with a net loss of $8.0 million, or $(3.08) per share, in the second quarter of 2026 and $9.3 million, or $(4.87) per share, in the first quarter of 2026, a 7% reduction from the first quarter to the third. The sequential change was driven entirely by interest expense of $5.7 million, of which approximately $4.1 million was non-cash amortization of debt discount and issuance costs associated with the Company’s 2026 financings. Interest expense and depreciation and amortization, which together totaled $6.4 million, represented approximately 73% of the third quarter net loss.

 

EBITDA improved for the second consecutive quarter, to $(2.3) million from $(4.4) million in the second quarter and $(7.2) million in the first quarter of 2026, a 68% improvement from the first quarter to the third, reflecting growing revenue, expanding gross margin and the absence of the derivative and settlement charges recorded in the first half. Adjusted EBITDA, which excludes those charges, was $(2.2) million in the third quarter, compared with $(1.9) million in the second quarter and $(1.6) million in the first quarter of 2026, reflecting higher corporate and public-company expenses, which more than offset improved results in both operating segments. See “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below for definitions and reconciliations.

 

Segment Results

 

Logistics. Logistics revenue was $3.39 million, up 5.8% sequentially, with hauling revenue of $3.23 million, up 5.7%, reflecting route density and contracted volumes added over the course of 2026. Gross margin expanded to 25.3% from 21.7% in the second quarter, and operating income rose 61% sequentially to $0.42 million, the segment’s third consecutive quarter of operating profitability.

 

Compost Sales. Compost Sales revenue was $1.09 million, up 3.9% sequentially, at a 62.1% gross margin, and the segment’s operating loss narrowed 16% to $(0.77) million. The segment’s gross margin above 60% reflects the unit economics of the Myakka City platform at its current scale; the Microtec mill described below is designed to expand the volume and range of products that platform can sell into higher-value end markets beginning in 2027.

 

Microtec Mill Program Update

 

Construction and installation of the Microtec mill at the Company’s permitted 80+ acre organics processing facility in Myakka City, Florida was completed in late October 2026. Remaining steps to bring the mill online are electrical energization, installation of the fire suppression system and commissioning. The Company’s electrical plans are being stamped by its electrical engineer in support of the electrical permit application, and the Company expects the mill to be energized before the end of 2026, with first substrate production targeted for January 2027 and a phased ramp of production volumes planned through the first half of 2027.

 

Through September 30, 2026, the Company had invested approximately $1.8 million of the approximately $2.0 million total program budget, with approximately $1.7 million capitalized on the balance sheet. The Company believes the mill will be the only installation of its kind in Florida.

 

The mill is manufacturer-rated at a throughput of up to 10 tons per hour. Actual production volumes will depend on feedstock mix, moisture content, operating schedule and the results of commissioning, and the Company’s initial 2027 production plan contemplates a phased, single-shift ramp well below rated capacity, leaving substantial headroom to scale output as demand develops. The Company expects to begin reporting substrate production and ramp metrics with its fourth quarter and full year 2026 results, which the Company expects to report following first production.

The mill is designed to precisely size, refine and condition organic inputs already aggregated at Myakka City into engineered growing media with repeatable quality and defined specifications for the horticultural market. Florida’s nursery and horticulture industry today relies substantially on growing media produced out of state and transported into the region, and rising freight costs continue to increase the delivered cost of those products. RenX intends to produce substrate locally, at the source of its feedstock, pairing manufactured-product margins with the Company’s existing inbound material flows.

 

Fiscal 2027 Outlook

 

2026 established the trajectory: revenue up every quarter, gross margin above 30% throughout, a profitable Logistics segment, net loss reduced 7% and EBITDA improved 68% from the first quarter to the third (see “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” below). The Company’s objective for 2027 is to repeat that pattern at larger scale and add a new revenue engine to it. With the Microtec mill moving from construction to commissioning, fiscal 2027 is expected to be the Company’s first year of manufactured substrate revenue, a product line that did not exist in any prior period.

 

The mill will change the revenue model at Myakka City. Today the platform earns revenue twice on the material it handles: fees on inbound material received from third-party tree services, landscape companies and haulers, and sales of bulk compost, soils and mulch on the way out. Substrate will add a third, higher-value layer by converting a portion of that same feedstock, already received and already on site, into engineered growing media that management expects to carry higher price points per yard than the Company’s bulk products. Because the input material and the processing platform are already in place, management expects incremental substrate revenue to carry manufactured-product margins. The market the mill addresses is substantial: Florida’s greenhouse, nursery and floriculture industry supports more than $4 billion in annual sales statewide, according to UF/IFAS, and is today supplied substantially by growing media produced out of state and freighted into the region.

 

Management’s operating priorities for 2027 are:

 

●Commission the Microtec mill and ramp substrate production through the first half of 2027, with substrate expected to become the Company’s highest-gross-margin product line as volumes scale;

 

●Accelerate Compost Sales segment revenue growth on the substrate ramp and reach operating cash flow breakeven in the segment during the first half of 2027, supported by a focused cost and fleet optimization program at Myakka City, converting the segment from an investment phase into a self-funding operation;

 

●Advance sales of the Company’s legacy real estate assets, including the previously announced agreement by the Norman Berry joint venture, in which the Company holds a 50% interest, to sell the Norman Berry property for $2.6 million, which, if completed, would convert non-core real estate into non-dilutive funding for the Myakka City platform (Closing remains subject to the conditions described in the Company’s August 2026 announcement).

 

Achieving these objectives will require additional capital, which may not be available on acceptable terms, if at all. There is no guarantee we will be successful in raising capital outside of our current sources. In addition, under the purchase agreements from our recent private placement offerings, we are subject to certain restrictive covenants that may make it difficult for us to procure additional financing. We expect that we will need additional future financing which may not be available on acceptable terms, if at all.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

This press release includes EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA, which are financial measures that are not calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”). The Company defines EBITDA as net income (loss) before interest expense, income taxes, depreciation and amortization, and Adjusted EBITDA as EBITDA further adjusted for the items identified in the reconciliation tables above, which management believes are not indicative of the Company’s core operating performance. These measures are presented as supplements to, and should not be considered substitutes for or superior to, the comparable GAAP measures, including net loss, which is presented first in this release. The Company’s non-GAAP measures may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies. No income tax effect has been applied to the adjustments because the Company recorded no income tax expense in the periods presented.

 

Management believes EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA are useful to investors because they facilitate period-over-period comparison of the Company’s operating performance by excluding the effects of the Company’s capital structure (interest expense, including non-cash amortization of debt discount and issuance costs) and non-cash depreciation and amortization, and, in the case of Adjusted EBITDA, items that management does not consider indicative of core operating performance: the change in fair value and loss on settlement of a derivative liability, losses on exchanges of debt obligations, an advisory fee and settlement-related expenses, non-cash stock issued for services, and gains and losses on sales of equipment. Management also uses these measures for internal planning and budgeting purposes and in its reporting to the Company’s board of directors.

 

The Company’s target of reaching operating cash flow breakeven in the Compost Sales segment is a forward-looking non-GAAP objective. The Company is unable to provide a reconciliation of this forward-looking measure to the most directly comparable GAAP measure without unreasonable effort because of the inherent difficulty of forecasting the timing and amounts of the reconciling items, including [the timing of substrate revenue, working capital changes and capital expenditures at the segment level]. The unavailable information could have a significant impact on the Company’s future GAAP results.

 

About RenX Enterprises Corp.

 

RenX Enterprises Corp. is a technology-driven environmental processing and sustainable materials company focused on producing value-added compost, engineered soils, and specialty growing media for agricultural, commercial, and consumer end markets. The Company’s platform is designed to be differentiated by its use of advanced milling and material-processing technology, including a planned deployment of a licensed Microtec system, to precisely size, refine, and condition organic inputs into consistent, high-performance soil substrates. This technology-enabled approach will allow RenX to move beyond traditional waste-to-value operations and manufacture engineered growing media with repeatable quality and defined specifications.

 

RenX’s core operations are anchored by a permitted 80+ acre organics processing facility in Myakka City, Florida. At this facility, the Company integrates organics processing, advanced milling, blending, and in-house logistics to support the localized production of proprietary soil substrates and potting media. The Company believes that by optimizing products for regional feedstocks and customer requirements, it can shorten supply chains, enhance quality control, and improve unit economics while serving higher-value end markets. The Company also owns a portfolio of legacy real estate assets, which it intends to monetize to fund its core technology-driven environmental processing platform.
