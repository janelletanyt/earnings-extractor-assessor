California Resources Corporation Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Results

Raised Quarterly Dividend by 4% and Delivered 2% Quarter-over-Quarter Net Production Growth

Sharpened Focus on California through Uinta Basin Sale and Closing of Crimson Midstream Acquisition

LONG BEACH, Calif., Nov. 2, 2026 - California Resources Corporation (NYSE: CRC) today reported its financial and operating results for the third quarter of 2026. The Company plans to host a conference call and webcast at 1 p.m. ET (10 a.m. PT) on Tuesday, November 3, 2026. Conference call details can be found within this release.

Third Quarter Highlights

•Delivered average net production of 152 thousand barrels of oil equivalent per day (MBoe/d) (80% oil)
•Reported a net loss of $315 million which includes the non-cash loss of $540 million from changes in the fair value of outstanding commodity derivatives, adjusted net income1 of $104 million and $341 million of adjusted EBITDAX1 which exceeded quarterly guidance
•Generated net cash provided by operating activities of $305 million, deployed $158 million of total capital and delivered $147 million of free cash flow1
•Returned $66 million to shareholders, including $36 million in dividends and $30 million in share repurchases2
•Increased quarterly dividend by 4%, reinforcing commitment to sustainable shareholder returns; declared quarterly dividend of $0.42/share to be paid in the fourth quarter of 20262
•Ended the quarter with $1,280 million in borrowing capacity and $35 million in available cash and cash equivalents3, representing $1,315 million of liquidity1, 3

Other Highlights

•Announced a memorandum of understanding4 (MOU) with Bloom Energy to jointly evaluate and target the potential development of 1 gigawatt (GW) or more of solid-oxide fuel cell power generation paired with carbon capture and storage. See California Resources Corporation Announces Strategic Collaboration with Bloom Energy to Advance Decarbonized Power Across California for additional information
•Signed agreement to divest its Uinta Basin (Utah) assets for total cash consideration of approximately $90 million, subject to customary purchase price adjustments. The transaction is expected to close by year-end, subject to certain third-party consents and other customary closing conditions. See California Resources Corporation Announces Divestiture of Uinta Basin Assets for additional information

•Closed its all-cash acquisition of Crimson Midstream Holdings, LLC (“Crimson”), strengthening CRC’s Leading Energy Infrastructure Platform and expanding options for potential longer-term development of CO₂ transportation across California. See California Resources Corporation Completes Acquisition of Crimson for additional information
•Published the 2025 Sustainability Update providing an overview of CRC's sustainability efforts and performance

"Our team continues to execute exceptionally well through strong operational performance, synergy realization from our strategic initiatives and impressive free cash flow”, said Francisco Leon, CRC’s President and Chief Executive Officer. “Through continued capital discipline and the ongoing focus on sustainable efficiency gains, we are improving free cash flow and our liquidity profile. In addition, we remain committed to our shareholder capital return program with share repurchases and dividends, the latter demonstrated by the 4% dividend per share increase we announced today. We have also made significant progress on strategic priorities, including the Crimson pipeline acquisition, along with the Uinta Basin asset sale and Bloom Energy partnership. These initiatives broaden and strengthen our commitment to the Golden State while continuing to deliver value to all CRC constituents including our shareholders and California communities.”

Third Quarter 2026 Results

•Net NGL and natural gas production exceeded guidance by approximately 1 MBoe/d, supported by the new Belridge-Elk Hills connection and California’s first carbon capture and storage (CCS) project at CRC’s Elk Hills Cryogenic Gas Plant, which led to increased gas processing and NGL recovery
•Electricity revenue net of electricity generation expenses exceeded guidance primarily due to higher prices for resource adequacy revenue and sales from electricity delivered to the wholesale market as Elk Hills power plant ran at a higher operational capacity
•Operating expenses were higher than anticipated and increased over the prior period, primarily due to higher prices for natural gas purchased and used in steamflood operations, and higher workover activity

Shareholder Returns

CRC is committed to sustainably returning cash to shareholders through dividends and repurchases of its common stock. In the third quarter of 2026, CRC repurchased 0.6 million shares of its common stock for $30 million at an average price of $53.48 per share and returned $36 million in dividends to shareholders. Since 2021, the Company has returned approximately $1,721 million to shareholders, including $1,210 million in share repurchases and $511 million in dividends. As of September 30, 2026, CRC had $570 million remaining for share repurchases under its authorized Share Repurchase Program through December 31, 20272.

On November 2, 2026, CRC's Board of Directors amended its cash dividend policy to increase the anticipated total annual dividend by approximately 4% to $1.68, payable to shareholders in quarterly increments of $0.42 per share of common stock. The Board of Directors also declared a quarterly cash dividend of $0.42 per share of common stock, payable to shareholders of record on December 4, 2026. The dividend is expected to be paid on December 18, 20262.

Balance Sheet and Liquidity

As of September 30, 2026, CRC had $35 million in available cash and cash equivalents³, $1,280 million in available borrowing capacity under its Revolving Credit Facility (which reflects $1,460 million of borrowing capacity less $180 million of outstanding letters of credit) and liquidity of $1,315 million.

In October 2026, CRC entered into a new amendment to its Revolving Credit Facility to, among other things, extend the maturity date to March 2030, increase the elected commitments of its lenders to $1,470 million, re-affirm its borrowing base at $1,500 million, and implement certain other technical amendments.

About California Resources Corporation

California Resources Corporation (CRC) is an independent energy and carbon management company advancing the energy transition. CRC is committed to environmental stewardship while safely providing local, responsibly sourced energy. CRC is also focused on maximizing the value of its land, mineral ownership, and energy expertise for decarbonization by developing CCS and other emissions reducing projects. For more information about CRC, please visit www.crc.com.

About Carbon TerraVault

Carbon TerraVault (CTV), CRC’s carbon management business, develops services to capture, transport and permanently store carbon dioxide (CO2) for its customers. CTV is advancing a portfolio of carbon capture and storage (CCS) projects, including CTV I, which is now operational and injecting CO2 for permanent sequestration in a depleted reservoir deep underground. For more information, visit www.carbonterravault.com.

Management believes that the non-GAAP measures presented, when viewed in combination with CRC's financial and operating results prepared in accordance with GAAP, provide a more complete understanding of the factors and trends affecting the Company's performance. ADJUSTED EBITDAX
CRC defines adjusted EBITDAX as earnings before interest expense; income taxes; depreciation, depletion and amortization; exploration expense; other unusual, infrequent and out-of-period items; and other non-cash items.
