WILLSCOT REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

Exceeded Q3 2026 Outlook for Revenue and Adjusted EBITDA

Provides Early 2027 Outlook for Adjusted EBITDA Growth and Deleveraging Expectations

SCOTTSDALE, November 5, 2026 - WillScot Holdings Corporation (“WillScot” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: WSC), a leader in innovative temporary space solutions, today announced third quarter 2026 results, including key performance highlights and market updates, an update to its 2026 full year outlook, and early thoughts on 2027 Adjusted EBITDA growth and deleveraging.

Q3 20261
•Generated revenue of $601 million, gross profit margin percentage of 50.7%, and net income of $52 million.
•Reported Adjusted Net Income of $53 million and Adjusted EBITDA of $234 million at a 38.8% margin, up 160 basis points sequentially from the second quarter 2026.
•Reported diluted and Adjusted Diluted Earnings Per Share of $0.28 and $0.29, respectively.
•Generated leasing and services revenue of $573 million, up 7.5% year-over-year with a 4.0% increase in leasing revenue and a 22.7% increase in delivery and installation revenue.
•Modular space units-on-rent increased 1.2% year-over-year, accelerated by 12% year-over-year activation growth in the quarter.
•Generated Net cash provided by operating activities of $198 million, up 4% year-over-year, and Adjusted Free Cash Flow of $78 million.
•Invested $123 million in Net CAPEX, prioritizing new rental equipment and refurbishments in highly utilized product lines to support major project activity and Enterprise Accounts.
•Paid down $57 million of outstanding debt.
•Increased previously issued full year 2026 outlook for revenue and Net CAPEX to reflect increased commercial activity. Maintained the outlook for Adjusted EBITDA based on the expectation for sustained variable costs to support demand in the fourth quarter 2026 and into 2027.

Tim Boswell, President and Chief Executive Officer of WillScot, commented, "Our third quarter results exceeded our expectations for revenue and Adjusted EBITDA. Leasing revenue increased 4% and delivery and installation revenue increased 23% year-over-year. Modular activations increased year-over-year for the fourth consecutive quarter, driving a positive inflection in units-on-rent. And revenue from Enterprise Accounts was up 15% year-over-year in the quarter. Together, we believe these are strong indicators that momentum is building in the business, our differentiated value proposition is resonating in the market, and our revenue mix is shifting in favor of our highest value offerings with our top customers."

Boswell continued, "Margins and return on invested capital both increased sequentially in the quarter. We continue to see a higher mix of delivery, installation, and activation activity, which are short-term margin headwinds, but consistent with our increased volume growth expectations through the fourth quarter and into 2027. We continue to execute methodically across multiple levers in our field and centralized operations to further improve margins and the customer experience."

Boswell concluded, "We are supporting continued strong order activity with organic growth investments in our most highly utilized fleet categories where we see attractive returns and lease durations. These investments are strengthening our leasing revenue run-rate heading into next year, while continuing to generate strong free cash flow in the quarter. Overall, our improving run-rate, organic growth investments, and margin initiatives support mid-single digit percentage growth in Adjusted EBITDA in 2027. We believe this is a balanced initial outlook given potential risks to the pace of data center build-outs and potential upside if strength in modular activation activity continues or the demand profile broadens. We are excited about these fundamental improvements to our business trajectory and intend to execute against these opportunities on behalf of our shareholders."

Matt Jacobsen, Chief Financial Officer of WillScot, commented, "Third quarter 2026 revenues of $601 million and Adjusted EBITDA of $234 million exceeded our outlook due to continued strong leasing and services revenue growth. Adjusted EBITDA margins expanded 160 basis points sequentially in the third quarter while absorbing increased variable costs and higher-than-
1
expected delivery and installation revenues to support 12% year-over-year growth in modular unit activations. The acceleration of modular activation growth over the prior quarters resulted in an earlier-than-expected inflection to year-over-year modular units-on-rent growth in the third quarter."

Jacobsen continued, "Incorporating year-to-date 2026 results and ongoing activation levels, we are raising our 2026 outlook for revenue to $2.340 billion and maintaining Adjusted EBITDA at $920 million. Relative to our prior guidance, our expectations for modular activations and delivery and installation revenues in the fourth quarter have increased meaningfully. With these increases and the continued growth in our order book, we expect unit work order production levels to remain elevated. This combination is strengthening our leasing revenue run-rate heading into 2027 while reducing the seasonal sequential margin expansion that would be typical in the fourth quarter."

Jacobsen concluded, "Our improving operating trajectory and disciplined capital allocation provide us with both the opportunity to invest in growth and a clear path to reduce leverage, which remains a priority. As we balance absolute debt reduction with investments in attractive opportunities to drive Adjusted EBITDA growth, we expect leverage to be approaching 3.0x as we exit 2027."

About WillScot
WillScot (Nasdaq: WSC) is a leading provider of innovative turnkey space solutions in North America, helping customers keep projects moving and operations running. The Company partners with critical industries including construction, manufacturing, healthcare, government, energy and education to deliver the right solutions coupled with a high level of customer service. WillScot’s comprehensive portfolio of products – including modular complexes, dry and cold storage containers, blast-resistant buildings, clearspan industrial structures, fencing, and add-on furnishings and equipment – is customizable and flexible to support any project need. Headquartered in Scottsdale, Ariz., WillScot operates from a network of approximately 230 branch locations in the U.S., Canada, and Mexico.

Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA Margin
We define EBITDA as net income plus net interest (income) expense, income tax expense (benefit), depreciation and amortization. Our adjusted EBITDA ("Adjusted EBITDA") reflects the following further adjustments to EBITDA to exclude certain non-cash items and the effect of what we consider transactions or events not related to our core business operations:
•Currency (gains) losses, net on monetary assets and liabilities denominated in foreign currencies other than the subsidiaries’ functional currency.
•Restructuring costs, lease impairment expense, and other related charges associated with restructuring plans designed to streamline operations and reduce costs including employee and lease termination costs.
•Goodwill and other impairment charges related to non-cash costs associated with impairment charges to goodwill, other intangibles, rental fleet and property, plant and equipment.
•Costs to integrate acquired companies, including outside professional fees, non-capitalized costs associated with system integrations, non-lease branch and fleet relocation expenses, employee relocation and training costs, and other costs required to realize cost or revenue synergies.
•Transaction costs including legal and professional fees and other transaction specific related costs.
•Non-cash charges for stock compensation plans.
•Other expense, including consulting expenses related to certain one-time projects, financing costs not classified as interest expense, gains and losses on disposals of property, plant, and equipment, unrealized gains and losses on investments, costs to implement the Company's real estate exits prior to the approval of the Network Optimization Plan, and non-equity executive transition costs.
We define Adjusted EBITDA Margin as Adjusted EBITDA divided by revenue.
We evaluate business performance utilizing Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA Margin as shown in the reconciliations below. We believe that evaluating performance excluding such items noted above is meaningful because it provides insight with respect to the intrinsic and ongoing operating results of the Company and captures the business performance, inclusive of indirect costs. We believe that Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA Margin (as defined above) are useful to investors because they (i) allow investors to compare performance over various reporting periods on a consistent basis by removing from operating results the impact of items that do not reflect core operating performance; (ii) are used by our board of directors and management to assess our performance; (iii) may, subject to certain limitations, enable investors to compare the performance of the Company to its competitors; (iv) provide additional tools for investors to use in evaluating ongoing operating results and trends; and (v) align with definitions in our ABL Facility.

We include estimated taxes at our current statutory tax rate.
