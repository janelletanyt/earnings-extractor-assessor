Montauk Renewables Announces Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

PITTSBURGH, PENNSYLVANIA – November 16, 2026—Montauk Renewables, Inc. (“Montauk” or “the Company”) (NASDAQ: MNTK), a renewable energy company specializing in the management, recovery, and conversion of biogas into renewable natural gas (“RNG”), today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Third Quarter and Recent Highlights:

 

• Revenues of $50.2 million, increased 10.9% year-over-year

 

• Net income of $2.1 million, decreased 58.9% year-over-year

 

• Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA of $10.2 million, decreased 20.6% year-over-year

 

• RNG production of 1.5 million MMBtu, increased 6.7% year-over-year

 

• RINs from operations sold of 13.9 million, increased 12.2% year-over-year

 

• We have executed a non-binding term sheet with the lender of our New Senior Credit Facility for an amendment to borrow additional debt and temporarily forbear and standstill certain debt covenant requirements. As a result of management's updated assessment of the Company's liquidity and financial position, the financial statements included in the Company's Form 10-Q for the third quarter of 2026 do not include the previously anticipated going concern qualification discussed in the Company's Form 12b-25 filing.

 

In July 2026, we began generating power for sale from our Turkey, North Carolina facility and continued to generate power, RECs, biochar and biocrude at nominal levels throughout the third quarter of 2026. In August 2026, we received notice from our power purchase utility that the facility had received notice of commercial operation date. Our production of power and notice from the utility permitted us to generate both swine RECs and enhanced RECs in subsequent months. We identified specific programming modifications to our installed electrical switchgear which was completed during the third quarter of 2026. Also during our operations during the third quarter of 2026, we identified enhancements to address two specific concerns limiting the commercial production volumes of the facility, those being the moisture control of our inlet feedstock and the preservation of syngas temperature from our reactors to our compression and power generation. These concerns reduce the volume of feedstock that can be conveyed to our reactors, and increase the frequency of maintenance intervals to address the consequences of gas separation through the facility due to premature temperature loss. We anticipate these enhancements to take approximately three months to complete from the placement of componentry orders, and cost approximately $15.0 million. We expect to commence these enhancement projects immediately upon execution of the amendment to our New Senior Credit Facility.

Third Quarter Financial Results

 

Total revenues in the third quarter of 2026 were $50.2 million, an increase of $4.9 million (10.9%) compared to $45.3 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase is primarily related to environmental attribute revenues from the RINs distributed and sold from our GreenWave joint venture of approximately $4.0 million compared to $1.1 million in environmental attribute revenues related to RINs distributed and sold in the third quarter of 2025. Our third quarter of 2026 RNG volumes sold under fixed/floor-price contracts decreased approximately 73.9% compared to the third quarter of 2025 due to the expiration of fixed price pathway contracts. Our RNG commodity revenue decreased approximately 52.2% which was offset by an increase in RINs sold of 12.2%. Our RINs generated and unseparated decreased approximately 84.0% as a result of the transition to the Biogas Regulatory Reform Rule in 2025.

 

Operating and maintenance expenses for our RNG facilities in the third quarter of 2026 were $16.1 million, an increase of $2.2 million (15.4%) compared to $13.9 million in the third quarter of 2025. Our Apex facility operating and maintenance expenses increased approximately $1.0 million primarily related to increased media changes, waste disposal, flare rent, and utility costs. Our Rumpke facility operating and maintenance expenses increased approximately $0.5 million primarily related to increased media changes. Our McCarty facility operating and

maintenance expenses increased approximately $0.3 million primarily related to increased media changes and timing of gas processing equipment preventative maintenance. Our Renewable Electricity Generation operating and maintenance expenses in the third quarter of 2026 were $5.9 million, an increase of $3.3 million (126.3%) compared to $2.6 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase is driven by an increase in non-capitalizable operating costs of approximately $1.9 million at our Montauk Ag Renewables project. Our Bowerman facility operating and maintenance expenses increased approximately $0.9 million primarily related to increased wellfield operational costs and timing of electrical processing preventative maintenance. We recorded approximately $4.1 million of expenses in the third quarter of 2026 related to the cost of RINs distributed from GreenWave and the costs related to pathway dispensing associated with our dispensing RNG in exclusive unique and proprietary pathways. Total general and administrative expenses were $7.6 million in the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $1.1 million (16.4%) compared to $6.5 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase is primarily related to higher employee-related costs in the third quarter of 2026 which relate to the timing of incentive compensation accrual adjustments.

 

Operating loss in the third quarter of 2026 was $1.0 million compared to an operating income of $4.4 million in the third quarter of 2025, a decrease of approximately $5.4 million (121.7%). We recognized income of $2.6 million from our GreenWave joint venture in the third quarter of 2026. Net income in the third quarter of 2026 was $2.1 million, a decrease of $3.1 million (58.9%) compared to $5.2 million in the third quarter of 2025.

 

Third Quarter Operational Results

 

We produced 1.5 million Metric Million British Thermal Units (“MMBtu”) of RNG during the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 0.1 million MMBtu (6.7%) compared to 1.4 million MMBtu produced in the third quarter of 2025. Our McCarty facility produced 60 thousand MMBtu more in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025 as a result of landfill host wellfield operational and collection system enhancements. Our Rumpke facility produced 42 thousand MMBtu more in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025 as a result of expansion of the wellfield collection system. We produced approximately 41 thousand megawatt hours (“MWh”) in Renewable Electricity in the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of 3 thousand MWh (6.8%) compared to 44 thousand MWh produced in the third quarter of 2025. Our Tulsa facility produced approximately 1 thousand MWh less in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025 as a result of an engine repair that was completed in July 2026. Our Pico facility produced approximately 1 thousand MWh less in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025 as a result of a turbo repair that was completed in August 2026.

 

Liquidity and Capital

 

We have executed a non-binding term sheet with the lender of our New Senior Credit Facility for an amendment to borrow additional debt and temporarily forbear and standstill certain debt covenant requirements. As a result of management's updated assessment of the Company's liquidity and financial position, the financial statements included in the Company's Form 10-Q for the third quarter of 2026 do not include the previously anticipated going concern qualification discussed in the Company's Form 12b-25 filing. If we are unsuccessful in obtaining relief from the lenders under our New Senior Credit Facility, we expect to be in default of the covenants thereunder as of December 31, 2026.

 

2026 Full Year Outlook

•

The reduction in REG revenues and volumes outlook relates to our current expectations on revenue and REG generation at our Montauk Ag Renewables facility.

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

This press release and the accompanying tables include references to EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA, which are Non-GAAP financial measures. We present EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA because we believe the measures assist investors in analyzing our performance across reporting periods on a consistent basis by excluding items that we do not believe are indicative of our core operating performance.

 

In addition, EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA are financial measurements of performance that management and the board of directors use in their financial and operational decision-making and in the determination of certain compensation programs. EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA are supplemental performance measures that are not required by or presented in accordance with GAAP. EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered alternatives to net income (loss) or any other performance measure derived in accordance with GAAP, or as an alternative to cash flows from operating activities or a measure of our liquidity or profitability.

 

About Montauk Renewables, Inc.

 

Montauk Renewables, Inc. (NASDAQ: MNTK) is a renewable energy company specializing in the management, recovery and conversion of biogas into RNG. The Company captures methane, preventing it from being released into the atmosphere, and converts it into either RNG or electrical power for the electrical grid (“Renewable Electricity”). The Company, headquartered in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, develops, operates and manages landfill methane-fueled renewable energy projects. The Company has current operations at 13 operating projects and on going development projects located in California, Idaho, Ohio, Oklahoma, Pennsylvania, North Carolina, South Carolina, and Texas. The Company sells RNG and Renewable Electricity, taking advantage of Environmental Attribute premiums available under federal and state policies that incentivize their use. For more information, visit https://ir.montaukrenewables.com
