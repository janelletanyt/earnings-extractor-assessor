CVD Equipment Provides Strategic Update and Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

CENTRAL ISLIP, N.Y. (Business Wire) – November 5, 2026 – CVD Equipment Corporation (NASDAQ: CVV) (the “Company”) today provided an update on its go-forward business strategy and reported its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Strategic Update

 

●As previously announced, the Company will no longer pursue new system orders for its CVD Equipment business and continues to evaluate strategic alternatives.

 

●The Company has further reduced its workforce to a level aligned with completing the remaining equipment backlog, satisfying warranty obligations, and supporting its ongoing spare parts, quartz, and services business. The Company continues to make progress toward achieving these objectives.

 

●CBRE, Inc., a commercial real estate services firm, has been engaged to market the Company’s owned real estate properties in Central Islip and Saugerties, New York, for sale.

 

Financial Update

 

●The Company ended the quarter with approximately $23.5 million of cash and cash equivalents and no outstanding debt.

 

●Reflecting the $14.1 million net gain on the sale of SDC in the second quarter of 2026, the Company reported net income of $9.4 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Lawrence J. Waldman, CVD’s Chairman of the Board, commented: “With a strengthened balance sheet resulting from our sale of the SDC business and no outstanding debt, the Board continues to evaluate strategic alternatives and pursue the disciplined monetization of the Company’s assets. The engagement of CBRE to market our Central Islip and Saugerties properties represents another important step in that process.”

Warren Cheesman, Acting Chief Executive Officer of CVD Equipment Corporation, stated, “During the quarter, we took further actions to align our workforce and cost structure with the Company’s current operating requirements. We remain focused on completing the existing equipment backlog, satisfying warranty obligations, and supporting our ongoing spare parts, quartz, and services business.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 Performance

 

Balance Sheet

 

●Cash increased to $23.5 million from $8.7 million at December 31, 2025, reflecting proceeds from the SDC divestiture.

 

●Stockholders’ equity increased to $34.6 million at September 30, 2026 from $24.7 million at December 31, 2025.

 

Operations (excluding the discontinued operations of SDC)

 

●Bookings were $0.7 million, compared with $1.0 million in the third quarter of 2025, due to lower system orders.

 

●Revenue was $1.6 million, a decrease of 72.1% from the prior-year quarter, primarily reflecting lower system revenue from reduced bookings.

 

●Gross margin was 9.2% versus 32.0% in the prior-year quarter, principally due to lower system revenue and the resulting impact of fixed manufacturing costs on a reduced revenue base, partially offset by an increase in non-system revenue.

 

●Backlog was $3.0 million at September 30, 2026, as compared to $3.9 million at June 30, 2026.

 

●Restructuring charges were $0.8 million and consisted primarily of severance costs, asset impairments and legal and board-related costs associated with the Company’s decision to discontinue the pursuit of new system orders and reduce its workforce.

 

Net (Loss) Income

 

●Net loss from continuing operations was $2.1 million, or ($0.30) per basic and diluted share, compared with net income from continuing operations of $0.1 million, or $0.02 per basic and diluted share, in the prior-year quarter. The net loss in the current quarter includes the restructuring charge of $0.8 million.

●Net income from discontinued operations was $0.6 million, or $0.09 per basic and diluted share, primarily due to a change in the estimate of income taxes payable related to the gain on the sale of SDC. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, income from discontinued operations was $14.6 million, including a $14.1 million net gain on the SDC divestiture.

 

Investor Conference Calls

 

The Company is not holding an investor call this quarter and expects to hold investor calls in connection with significant corporate developments, as appropriate.

About CVD Equipment Corporation

 

CVD Equipment Corporation (NASDAQ: CVV) has designed, developed, and manufactured chemical vapor deposition, thermal processing, physical vapor transport, and related equipment and process solutions used in industrial and research applications. The Company continues to support existing customer projects and supplies quartz components, replacement parts, process support, and related services for its installed base.
