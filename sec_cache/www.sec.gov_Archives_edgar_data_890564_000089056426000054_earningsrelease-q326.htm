Everforth Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Continued Momentum with Revenues, Net Income, and Adjusted EBITDA Above Guidance
Adjusted EBITDA Margin at the Top End of the Guidance Range


October 28, 2026

RICHMOND, VA.— (BUSINESS WIRE) -- Everforth, Inc. (NYSE: EFOR), a leading technology and digital engineering company, reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Highlights

Third Quarter 2026
•Revenues were $1,031.4 million
•Net income was $23.1 million
•Adjusted EBITDA (a non-GAAP measure) was $106.3 million (10.3 percent of revenues)
•Operating cash flows were $58.7 million and Free Cash Flow (a non-GAAP measure) was $46.5 million
•On July 7, 2026, completed the refinancing and upsizing of revolver, replacing previous revolver and Term Loan A, with a new five-year $600 million revolving facility
•Reduced debt by $52.8 million, including payments of $43.8 million under the senior secured credit facility and $9.0 million of senior unsecured notes repurchased in the open market at a discount

IT Consulting Metrics
•     Commercial Segment - book-to-bill ratio for the IT Consulting business trailing-twelve-month period ("TTM") was 1.1 to 1
•     Federal Government Segment - new contract awards for the TTM were $1.0 billion and book-to-bill ratio was 0.9 to 1

Management Commentary

“Everforth’s third quarter 2026 results demonstrated continued momentum in the business. Revenues of $1.03 billion and Adjusted EBITDA of $106.3 million surpassed the high end of our guidance, while Adjusted EBITDA margin of 10.3 percent was at the top end of our guidance range,” said Ted Hanson, Chief Executive Officer of Everforth, Inc. “As we enter the final quarter of the year, strong Commercial and Federal bookings reflect continued customer demand for AI and data, cybersecurity, cloud, and IT modernization services.”

Mr. Hanson continued, “AI investment remains significant, but many of the most complex integration, security, and governance challenges are still ahead, particularly as AI agents become a greater part of how work gets done. Our strategic technology alliances, proven execution, and cross-sector expertise increasingly position Everforth as a forward deployed partner, giving us early access to new capabilities and the experience to put them to work in complex customer environments as enterprise AI scales.”

Consolidated revenues for the quarter were $1,031.4 million, up from $1,011.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. Commercial Segment revenues were 70 percent of total revenues and were $719.9 million, up from $711.3 million in the third quarter of 2025. Federal Government Segment revenues were 30 percent of total revenues and were $311.5 million, up from $300.1 million in the prior-year period.

Commercial Segment revenues are categorized into five industries: (i) Consumer and Industrial, (ii) Technology, Media and Telecom ("TMT"), (iii) Financial Services, (iv) Healthcare, and (v) Business Services. Year-over-year, TMT increased by $20.0 million, or 14.6 percent, and Business Services increased by $5.6 million, or 7.2 percent, and the remaining three industries decreased. Sequentially, TMT increased by $12.4 million, or 8.6 percent, and Business Services increased by $6.3 million, or 8.2 percent, and the remaining three industries were stable.

Federal Segment revenues are categorized into four customer types: (i) Defense and Intelligence, (ii) National Security, (iii) Federal Civilian, and (iv) other clients. National Security and Federal Civilian revenues increased year-over-year, partially offset by decreases in Defense and Intelligence and other clients.

Gross margin for the third quarter of 2026 was 28.3 percent, a compression of 110 basis points from the third quarter of 2025. Gross margin for the Commercial Segment was 31.9 percent, down 130 basis points year-over-year. Contributing to the compression was (i) a lower mix of high-margin permanent placement revenues, (ii) changes in foreign currency exchange rates primarily related to our delivery center in Mexico, and (iii) a shift in solutions mix, with stronger growth in certain cloud and infrastructure services that carry lower gross margins than other offerings in our portfolio. Gross margin for the Federal Government Segment was 19.8 percent, down 50 basis points year-over-year primarily due to an increase in revenues from cost reimbursable contracts which carry a lower margin than the other contract types.
Selling, general, and administrative (“SG&A”) expenses were $217.7 million, compared with $212.2 million in the prior-year period. SG&A expenses included $8.3 million in acquisition, integration, and strategic planning expenses, compared with $4.2 million in the prior-year period.

Net income was $23.1 million ($0.56 per diluted share), compared with $38.1 million ($0.87 per diluted share) in the third quarter of 2025.

Adjusted EBITDA (a non-GAAP measure) was $106.3 million, or 10.3 percent of revenues ("Adjusted EBITDA margin," a non-GAAP measure), compared with $112.6 million or 11.1 percent of revenues in the third quarter of 2025.

Capital Resources and Allocation

At September 30, 2026, the Company had:
•Cash and cash equivalents of $147.5 million
•Availability of approximately $227.0 million under the Company's $600.0 million senior secured revolving credit facility (due 2031)
•Term Loan B facility with outstanding balance of $485.0 million (due 2030)
•Senior unsecured notes totaling $541.0 million at 4.625 percent (due 2028)

During the quarter, the Company completed the refinancing and upsizing of its revolver, replacing the previous revolver and Term Loan A with a new five-year $600 million revolving credit facility, due in 2031. In addition, the Company reduced its debt balance by $52.8 million, including payments of $43.8 million under the senior secured credit facility and $9.0 million of senior unsecured notes repurchased at a discount.

There were no repurchases of the Company's common stock during the quarter; approximately $923 million remained available for repurchases under the Company's stock repurchase plan.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

Statements in this release include financial information presented in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States ("GAAP") and also include non-GAAP financial measures that are provided as additional information to enhance the overall understanding of the Company's current financial performance and not as an alternative to the consolidated interim financial statements presented in accordance with GAAP. Management uses these non-GAAP measures (earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization ("EBITDA"), Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA margin, Adjusted Net Income, Adjusted Net Income per diluted share, Free Cash Flow, and Revenues on a same Billable Days basis) to evaluate the Company's financial performance. These terms might not be calculated in the same manner as, and thus might not be comparable to, similarly titled measures reported by other companies. The financial information tables that accompany this press release include reconciliations of net income to non-GAAP financial measures.

EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, and Adjusted EBITDA margin provide a measure of the Company's operating results in a manner that is focused on the performance of the Company's core business on an ongoing basis, by removing the effects of non-operating and certain non-cash expenses. These non-operating and non-cash items are specifically identified in the reconciliations of GAAP measures to Non-GAAP measures that accompany this release.

Adjusted Net Income provides a method for assessing the Company's operating results in a manner that is focused on the performance of the Company's core business on an ongoing basis by removing the effects of non-operating and certain non-cash expenses on a net of tax basis. The metric is not adjusted by the benefit of the tax deduction associated with the amortization of acquired definite-lived intangible assets as these cash tax savings appropriately reflect the performance of the Company's acquisitions.

Free Cash Flow provides useful information to investors about the amount of cash generated by the business that can be used for strategic opportunities and is computed as presented in the tables that accompany this release.

IT Consulting Metrics

Commercial IT consulting book-to-bill ratio represents the ratio of consulting bookings to related revenues for a specified period. Commercial IT consulting accounts for approximately 50 percent of the segment’s revenues and has increased as a proportion of the segment’s revenues over time. Bookings represent the value of new contracts entered into during the period, including adjustments for changes in contract scope and contract terminations. Measuring bookings involves the use of estimates and judgments and there are no independent standards or requirements governing the calculation of bookings. There is no assurance these bookings will result in future revenues.

Federal Government Segment new contract awards are defined as the estimated amount of future revenues to be recognized under contracts awarded during a specified period, including adjustments to estimates for contracts awarded in previous periods. The book-to-bill ratio for the Federal Government Segment is the ratio of New Contract Awards to revenues for a specified period. There is no assurance our new contract awards will result in future revenues.
