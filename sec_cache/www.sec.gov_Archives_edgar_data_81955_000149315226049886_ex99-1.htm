Rand Capital Reports Third Quarter Fiscal Year 2026 Results

 

  ● Interest income from portfolio companies rose 4% sequentially to $1.2 million, supported by contributions from investments made earlier in 2026
     
  ● Third quarter total investment income was $1.3 million compared with $1.6 million in the prior-year period, primarily reflecting the impact of certain investments being on non-accrual status
     
  ● Net investment income was $669,000, or $0.23 per share, in the third quarter
     
  ● Net asset value (NAV) per share was $17.36 at September 30, 2026
     
  ● Portfolio fair value was $56.7 million, up 17.0% from year-end 2025

 

BUFFALO, NY, November 4, 2026 – Rand Capital Corporation (Nasdaq: RAND) (“Rand” or the “Company”), a business development company providing alternative financing for lower middle market companies, announced its results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“Interest income from our portfolio companies increased sequentially in the third quarter, supported by contributions from investments made earlier in the year and building on a relatively stable income base during 2026,” said Daniel P. Penberthy, President and Chief Executive Officer of Rand. “Improved operating performance at several portfolio companies also supported valuation gains, helping offset declines elsewhere and contributing to a modest sequential increase in net asset value per share.”

 

“Our focus remains on strengthening the portfolio’s income producing base, actively managing challenged investments and preserving financial flexibility. With a predominantly debt-oriented portfolio and available borrowing capacity, we intend to remain selective in committing capital, balancing new investment opportunities with the needs of our existing portfolio and long-term shareholder returns.”

 

Third Quarter Review (compared with the prior-year period unless otherwise noted)

 

  ● Total investment income of $1.3 million decreased $268,000, or 17%, from $1.6 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily reflecting lower interest income from portfolio companies due to certain investments being placed on non-accrual status over the last year, along with lower interest from other investments and fee income. Dividend income of $28,000 in the third quarter of 2026 partially offset this decline.
     
  ● On a sequential basis, interest income from portfolio companies increased 4% to $1.2 million, reflecting income generated by new investments made during the year.
     
  ● Non-cash payment-in-kind (PIK) interest totaled $99,000, representing 8% of interest income from portfolio companies, compared with 43% in the prior-year period. The decline in PIK as a percentage of interest income reflects the impact of four portfolio companies being on non-accrual status, which reduced the amount of PIK interest recognized in the quarter.
     
  ● Total expenses were $769,000 compared with $596,000 in the same period last year. The increase primarily reflected higher professional fees, interest expense due to higher average borrowings and base management fees, partly offset by lower income-based incentive fees. Neither period included a capital gains incentive fee expense or benefit.

  ● Net investment income was $669,000, or $0.23 per share, compared with $993,000, or $0.33 per share, in the third quarter of 2025.
     
  ● Net assets were $51.5 million, or $17.36 per share, at September 30, 2026, compared with $17.33 per share, at June 30, 2026. Net investment income of $669,000 and net realized and unrealized gains of $275,000 more than offset $861,000 of dividends declared during the third quarter. At December 31, 2025, net assets were $17.57 per share.

 

Portfolio and Investment Activity

 

As of September 30, 2026, Rand’s investment portfolio had a fair value of $56.7 million, up 17.0% from $48.5 million at December 31, 2025. The portfolio consisted of investments in 20 portfolio businesses and was comprised of approximately 77% debt investments and 23% equity investments at fair value. Portfolio fair value increased $235,000 from June 30, 2026.

 

Rand deployed $12.0 million into new and follow-on investments during the first nine months of 2026, with all of that deployment occurring in the first half of the year. No new or follow-on cash investments were made during the third quarter of 2026.

 

The annualized weighted average yield of debt investments, including PIK interest, was 8.97% at September 30, 2026, compared with 11.3% at December 31, 2025. This change in the annualized weighted average yield primarily reflects the timing of placing certain portfolio investments on non-accrual status.

 

Third Quarter 2026 portfolio activity included:

 

  ● BMP Swanson Holdco LLC: Rand recognized a $2.0 million realized loss on its debt and equity investments, which had previously been written down to zero. Recognition of the realized loss was accompanied by an equivalent reversal of previously recorded unrealized depreciation and therefore did not result in an additional reduction in NAV during the quarter.
     
  ● BlackJet Direct Marketing LLC: Rand received a $250,000 principal repayment on its debt investment and recognized a $7,500 prepayment fee.
     
  ● Feature Healthcare LLC, doing business as 4 Seasons Home Care: Rand received a $16,250 principal repayment.
     
  ● Valuation increases in Bauer Sheet Metal and Fabricating, Inter-National Electronic Alloys and Open Exchange were partly offset by decreases in Autotality, ITA Acquisition and OnCore Golf.

 

Liquidity and Capital Resources

 

Rand ended the quarter with $223,000 in cash and cash equivalents on hand, compared with $4.2 million at December 31, 2025. The Company had $5.0 million outstanding on its line of credit as of September 30, 2026, with estimated remaining availability of approximately $12.4 million. The applicable borrowing rate at quarter-end was 7.4%.

 

The Company did not repurchase any outstanding common stock during the third quarter of 2026.

 

Dividends

 

Rand paid its regular quarterly cash dividend of $0.29 per share on September 9, 2026, to shareholders of record as of August 26, 2026. The dividend totaled approximately $861,000.

ABOUT RAND CAPITAL

 

Rand Capital Corporation (Nasdaq: RAND) is an externally managed business development company (BDC). The Company’s investment objective is to maximize total return to its shareholders with current income and capital appreciation by focusing its debt and related equity investments in privately-held, lower middle market companies with committed and experienced managements in a broad variety of industries. Rand primarily invests in businesses that have sustainable, differentiated and market-proven products, revenue of more than $10 million and EBITDA in excess of $1.5 million. The Company’s investment activities are managed by its external investment adviser, Rand Capital Management, LLC. Additional information can be found at the Company’s website where it regularly posts information: randcapital.com.
