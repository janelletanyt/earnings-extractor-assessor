TELA Bio Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Reduced sequential operating loss by 21%, including cost of realignment

 

MALVERN, PA, November 10, 2026 -- TELA Bio, Inc. (“TELA Bio”), a commercial-stage medical technology company focused on providing innovative soft-tissue reconstruction solutions, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Recent Highlights

 

●Delivered $19.0 million revenue in the third quarter of 2026;
●Increased international revenue by 18% over the prior year period;
●Loss from operations decreased by $2.0 million or 21% sequentially while Adjusted EBITDA loss decreased by $4.3 million or 55% sequentially;
●Announced and implemented a strategic cost reduction initiative to reduce annual operating expenses by $17.0 million and extend cash runway into 2028;
●Regained compliance with Nasdaq minimum bid price requirement for continued listing on the Nasdaq Global Market;
●Received 510(k) clearance for Liora Monofilament Scaffold for soft tissue reinforcement; and
●Four peer-reviewed clinical results demonstrating favorable outcomes for OviTex and OviTex PRS.

 

“My first quarter as CEO was focused on putting a plan in place to return TELA Bio to growth. I joined because I believe OviTex is best-in-class technology and that we are well positioned to win in an evolving soft tissue repair and reconstruction market,” said Heather Getz, Chief Executive Officer of TELA Bio. “Our cost reduction initiative allows us to thoughtfully reallocate resources, supports a return to topline growth, and extends our cash runway. In the third quarter alone, we reduced our adjusted EBITDA loss by 55% versus the second quarter of 2026 while delivering sequential revenue growth in the US hernia market and continued year-over-year growth in Europe. Clinically, we continued to demonstrate the strength in our portfolio with four new peer-reviewed publications and podium presentations on both OviTex and OviTex PRS demonstrating favorable outcomes compared to gold standard synthetic and biologic alternatives. Commercially, we gained market share across the OviTex hernia portfolio, which gives us conviction that our products deliver significant value for patients, clinicians and hospital administrators. Looking ahead, we see ample opportunity for growth, and our team is focused on executing our long-term strategy.”

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Revenue was $19.0 million in the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of 8% compared to $20.7 million in the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was primarily driven by a decline in OviTex PRS volumes partially offset by growing international sales.

 

Gross profit was $12.3 million, or 64.9% of revenue, in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $14.0

million, or 67.5% of revenue, in the same period in 2025. The decrease in gross margin was primarily due to a higher charge for excess and obsolete inventory as a percentage of revenue. After adjusting for the $1.1 million of noncash charges gross margin1 would be 71%.

 

 

1 The Company believes that providing non-GAAP financial measures offers a meaningful representation of our performance, as we exclude expenses that are not necessary to support our ongoing business. Total operating expenses excluding other expenses was $17.6 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $21.4 million in the third quarter of 2025, a decrease of 17.5%.

 

Loss from operations was $7.3 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared to $7.6 million in the same period in 2025. Adjusted EBITDA loss was $3.4 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $5.7 million in the third quarter of 2025, an improvement of 39%.

 

On a GAAP basis, net loss was $9.3 million in the third quarter of 2026, or $0.16 per common share, compared to a net loss of $8.6 million, or $0.19 per common share in the same period in 2025.

 

On an adjusted basis, net loss for the third quarter of 2026 was $7.3 million, or $0.13 per common share, compared to a net loss of $8.4 million, or $0.18 per common share, in the same period in 2025. The details regarding adjusted net loss are included in the reconciliation tables in this release.

 

Cash and cash equivalents on September 30, 2026 were $22.7 million.

About TELA Bio, Inc.

 

TELA Bio, Inc. (NASDAQ: TELA) is a commercial-stage medical technology company focused on providing innovative technologies that optimize clinical outcomes by prioritizing the preservation and restoration of the patient’s own anatomy. The Company is committed to providing surgeons with advanced, economically effective soft-tissue reconstruction solutions that leverage the patient’s natural healing response while minimizing long-term exposure to permanent synthetic materials. For more information, visit www.telabio.com.

Statement Regarding Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

In this press release, the Company’s financial results are provided in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (GAAP) and using certain non-GAAP financial measures, including adjusted net loss and adjusted net loss per common share, adjusted EBITDA, and adjusted gross margin. Management believes that presentation of operating results using these non-GAAP financial measures provides useful supplemental information to investors and facilitates the analysis of the Company’s core operating results and comparison of operating results across reporting periods, while excluding certain expenses that may not be indicative of the Company’s recurring core business operating results. In addition, management believes these non-GAAP financial measures are useful to investors both because they (1) allow for greater transparency with respect to key metrics used by management in its financial and operational decision-making and (2) are used by the Company’s institutional investors and the analyst community to help them analyze the health of TELA’s business. Management also uses non-GAAP financial measures to establish budgets and to manage the Company’s business. A reconciliation of the GAAP financial results to non-GAAP financial results is included in the schedules below and a description of the adjustments made to the GAAP financial measures is included at the end of the schedules.

 

The Company encourages investors to carefully consider its results under GAAP, as well as its supplemental non-GAAP information and the reconciliation between these presentations, to more fully understand its business. Non-GAAP financial results are reported in addition to, and not as a substitute for, or superior to, financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP. Further, non-GAAP financial measures, even if similarly titled, may not be calculated in the same manner by all companies, and therefore should not be compared.
